## 1. Brand-Wide Content Strategy

### Core Messaging Framework

| Element | Expression |
|---------|-----------|
| **Brand Promise** | "Michelin-level dining, exclusively yours" |
| **Tone** | Confident, warm, understated luxury. Never ostentatious. |
| **Voice** | First-person plural ("our chefs," "we create"). Personal but professional. |
| **Vocabulary** | Curated, bespoke, artisanal, exclusively, sourced, composed, paired, handcrafted |
| **Avoid** | "Cheap," "discount," "budget," "best price," generic superlatives |
| **Price Positioning** | Premium-transparent. State investment levels, not hiding costs. |

### Universal Page Structure (Applied to All 5 Pages)

Every department page follows this structural template:

```
1. HERO (100vh) — Emotional hook + immediate CTA
2. TRUST BAR — Stats, badges, media logos (sticky or inline)
3. SERVICE DETAILS — What you get, inclusions, packages
4. PROCESS — How it works (4-step visual journey)
5. SOCIAL PROOF — Testimonials + media + case study
6. FAQ — Top 10 questions (expandable accordion)
7. PRICING/CTA — Investment guide + booking action
8. RELATED SERVICES — Cross-promo cards (3 departments)
9. FINAL CTA — Closing conversion block
```

### 2.6 FAQ SECTION — Top 10 Questions

**Accordion format, expandable**

1. **"How far in advance do I need to book?"**
   > We recommend 3-7 days for optimal menu planning. However, we accommodate last-minute requests within 48 hours when possible. High season (July-August, December) books 2-3 weeks ahead.

2. **"What is included in the price?"**
   > All food ingredients, chef and service team travel to your villa, complete table styling (linens, candles, glassware), full kitchen setup and cleanup, and of course the multi-course dining experience. Wine pairing is an optional add-on. Service charge and government tax are additional.

3. **"Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?"**
   > Absolutely. Every menu is composed from scratch for your party. We handle vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, kosher, nut allergies, and any other requirements. Your dietary consultation happens 3-5 days before your dinner.

4. **"Do I need a professional kitchen?"**
   > No. Our chefs are experienced in working with villa kitchens of all sizes. We bring any specialized equipment needed. A brief kitchen assessment is part of your pre-dinner consultation.

5. **"How long does the experience last?"**
   > The Essential menu is approximately 2.5 hours. The Signature menu is approximately 3 hours. We pace each course to your preference — some guests prefer a leisurely evening, others a more dynamic experience.

6. **"Can I see the menu before booking?"**
   > Yes. After you inquire with your date and guest count, we share sample menus based on your preferences. The final menu is confirmed during your chef consultation. Nothing is set until you approve it.

7. **"Is wine pairing included?"**
   > Wine pairing is optional. We offer two tiers: Classic Pairing (IDR 850K/person) with 4 wines, and Premium Pairing (IDR 1.1M/person) with 5 wines including Champagne. Non-alcoholic pairing is also available.

8. **"What areas of Bali do you serve?"**
   > We serve all major villa areas: Seminyak, Canggu, Uluwatu, Jimbaran, Nusa Dua, Ubud, Sanur, and Seseh. Travel is included for locations within 45 minutes of our base. Extended travel to remote areas may incur a surcharge.

9. **"What if I need to cancel or reschedule?"**
   > Cancellations made 7+ days before receive a full refund. 3-7 days: 50% refund. Less than 72 hours: the booking is non-refundable but can be rescheduled once within 30 days.

10. **"Can we add cocktail service or a live musician?"**
    > Yes. We coordinate cocktail receptions (additional IDR 350K/person for 2 signature cocktails) and can recommend trusted musicians — from acoustic guitar to string duos. Mention this when booking.

### 3.6 FAQ SECTION — Top 10 Questions

1. **"How does pricing work? Is it per person or per day?"**
   > Pricing is a flat daily rate based on your group size and meal plan. For example, our Breakfast + Dinner plan for a couple starts at IDR 1,800,000/day. For families of 4-6, the Full Board plan starts at IDR 2,400,000/day. We'll provide an exact quote based on your villa, group size, and duration.

2. **"Can the chef cook different meals for adults and children?"**
   > Absolutely. Our chefs are experienced in preparing separate kid-friendly meals — from simple pasta to creative plates that get children excited about food. This is included at no extra charge.

3. **"What if we want to eat out some nights?"**
   > No problem. You only pay for the days you use the service. Just give your chef 24 hours' notice if you plan to skip a meal or dine out. We understand flexibility is part of your holiday.

4. **"Who does the grocery shopping?"**
   > Your chef handles all ingredient sourcing. They shop at local markets and premium suppliers each morning, selecting the freshest produce, seafood, and meats. You simply reimburse the ingredient cost (typically IDR 400K-800K/day depending on your menu preferences). We provide full receipts.
