## Types of Events: Weddings, Corporate Retreats, Private Celebrations

Bali's infinity-pool villas and year-round weather make it a prime destination for hosted events. The key is understanding logistics: vendor coordination, cultural considerations, and weather contingencies.

### Weddings (Intimate to 200+ Guests)

**Small Weddings (10–40 guests)**
- Typically held in villa garden or beachfront venue
- Personal, customizable atmosphere
- Catering: 1–2 chefs, 2–3 staff members
- Timeline: 3–4 months planning
- Budget: $3,000–15,000 (highly variable by guest count and formality)

**Medium Weddings (50–100 guests)**
- May require rented pavilion or two-villa coordination
- More formal seating and service
- Catering: 2–3 chefs, 4–6 staff members
- Timeline: 4–6 months planning
- Budget: $15,000–50,000

**Large Weddings (100–200+ guests)**
- Professional event venue rental (hotel ballroom, dedicated wedding resort)
- Full event coordination team
- Catering: 5+ chefs, 10+ staff
- Timeline: 6–12 months planning
- Budget: $50,000–200,000+

### Corporate Retreats (15–50 Participants)

**Team-Building Retreats (3–5 days)**
- Mix of meetings, outdoor activities, group meals
- Catering: Breakfast (casual), lunch (varied options), dinner (themed)
- Staffing: Chefs, servers, activity coordinators
- Focus: Energy management, dietary accommodations for diverse diets
- Budget: $150–300 per person per day

**Executive Conferences (1–3 days)**
- Meeting infrastructure (WiFi, A/V, breakout rooms)
- Meals: Keynote breakfasts, catered lunches, networking dinners
- Staffing: Dedicated event coordinator, multiple chefs for volume
- Focus: Professional atmosphere with leisure elements
- Budget: $200–400 per person per day

**Wellness Retreats (5–7 days)**
- Yoga, meditation, spa, healthy cuisine focus
- Catering: Organic, plant-forward, specialty diets (vegan, raw, etc.)
- Staffing: Wellness instructors, nutrition-focused chefs
- Focus: Health transformation, community building
- Budget: $250–500 per person per day

### Private Celebrations (Birthdays, Anniversaries, Family Reunions)

**Milestone Birthdays (20–50 guests)**
- Casual villa gathering or formal dinner party
- Catering: 1–2 chefs, flexible menu
- Ambiance: Personalized; often includes photo display, music playlist
- Budget: $1,000–10,000 depending on scale

**Anniversaries (2–20 guests)**
- Romantic fine dining, intimate group, or private villa dinner
- Catering: Specialist chef, wine pairing
- Ambiance: Candlelit, personalized storytelling through menu
- Budget: $500–5,000

**Family Reunions (15–50 guests)**
- Multi-day villa stay or beachfront gathering
- Catering: Buffet or BBQ style, large quantities
- Activities: Bonding, games, group meals
- Budget: $2,000–15,000

## Venue Selection: Villa, Beach, Garden Setup

### Private Villa (Most Popular)

**Advantages:**
- Intimate, personalized
- All-in-one accommodation + event space
- Kitchen access for custom catering
- Privacy (no strangers observing)
- Flexible timing (no venue rental hours)

**Disadvantages:**
- Limited guest capacity (typically 2–4 bedroom villas fit 20–40 people comfortably)
- Weather risk (indoor backup required)
- Setup coordination with villa staff

**Best for:** Weddings under 50 guests, intimate celebrations, multi-day events

### Beach Venue (Sunset Views)

**Advantages:**
- Photographic backdrop (sunset, ocean)
- Flexible setup (tables on sand, ceremony on water)
- Professional catering access

**Disadvantages:**
- Tide and weather contingencies
- Sand logistics (tables sink, decorations blow)
- Limited shelter without tent rental
- Noise restrictions (some beaches regulated)

**Best for:** Afternoon/evening celebrations, 20–100 guests, weddings with photographic focus

### Villa Garden + Pavilion (Optimal Balance)

**Setup:**
- Garden ceremony or cocktails (open air, scenic)
- Pavilion or covered tent for dining (weather protection)
- Villa kitchens for food prep

**Advantages:**
- Weather flexibility (tent protects meal, but ceremony in garden if clear)
- Intimate feel with event infrastructure
- Elegant yet relaxed

**Best for:** Weddings 40–100 guests, corporate retreats, multi-activity events

## Catering & Menu Planning for Events

### Catering Team Structure

**For 20–50 guests:**
- 1–2 chefs
- 2–3 service staff
- 1 person for setup/breakdown

**For 50–100 guests:**
- 2–3 chefs
- 4–6 service staff
- 1–2 coordinators

**For 100+ guests:**
- 4–6 chefs (main + pastry + specialty)
- 8–12 service staff
- 2–3 event coordinators
- Separate bar manager

### Menu Planning by Event Type

**Wedding Menu** (3–5 courses, plated or family-style)
- Appetizer course (1 option or 2 choices)
- Entrée (2 choices typically: protein + vegetarian)
- Dessert (1–2 options)
- Coffee/tea
- Cost: $100–200 per person

**Corporate Retreat Menu** (Casual, accommodating diverse diets)
- Breakfast: Buffet with hot and cold options, fruit, breads
- Lunch: Two entrée choices + vegetarian, salads, sides
- Dinner: Themed (Balinese, BBQ, fusion) with options
- Cost: $40–80 per person per day

**Birthday/Anniversary Menu** (Fun, personalized)
- Tailored to honoree's preferences
- May include signature dish or nostalgia element
- Dessert: Custom cake or special plating
- Cost: $80–150 per person

## Staffing: Chefs, Waiters, Butlers, Event Coordinators

### Roles & Responsibilities

**Event Coordinator**
- Logistics (venue, timeline, vendor coordination)
- Guest flow and pacing
- Problem-solving day-of
- Typical fee: $300–1,000 (depends on event complexity)

**Executive Chef**
- Menu design, source planning, cost estimation
- Kitchen management and food prep
- Quality control on all dishes
- Typical cost: $500–2,000 per event (included in catering quotes)

**Sous Chef / Assistant**
- Prep work, supporting chef, plating
- Typical cost: $100–300 per day

**Pastry Chef** (For weddings, multi-course events)
- Dessert, breads, petit fours
- Typical cost: $200–500 per event

**Service Staff**
- Table service, water refills, clearing
- Ratio: 1 server per 8–10 guests for formal dinner
- Typical cost: $50–150 per person per event

**Butler** (For high-end events)
- Personalized guest service, bar management
- Typical cost: $150–300 per day

### Staffing Tips

1. **Hire through established hospitality services** (easier coordination, trained staff)
2. **Confirm final head count 1 week prior** (critical for staffing ratios)
3. **Brief all staff on dietary restrictions** (server errors cause guest issues)
4. **Have backup staff on standby** (someone may not show)
5. **Provide clear timeline** (when setup starts, when service begins, when breakdown)

## Timeline: Planning 3 Months Ahead vs. Last-Minute Events

### Ideal Timeline (3 Months Ahead)

**Month 1 (90 days before)**
- Secure venue and key vendors (caterer, coordinator)
- Define guest list and dietary needs
- Menu ideation with chef

**Month 2 (60 days before)**
- Finalize menu and confirm pricing
- Arrange catering contract
- Send guest invitations with RSVP deadline
- Book bar, photography, music if needed

**Month 3 (30 days before)**
- Confirm final head count (72 hours before is ideal)
- Finalize seating chart and logistics
- Brief all staff on dietary, cultural, logistical details
- Plan weather contingencies (pavilion setup, indoor alternatives)

### Contingency Timeline (6–8 Weeks)

- Limits customization but doable
- Vendor availability may be tighter
- Menu must be simpler (less sourcing time)

### Last-Minute Events (1–2 Weeks)

- Premium surcharges likely (20–50%)
- Limited chef availability (top chefs book far ahead)
- Menu must be straightforward (local, readily available ingredients)
- Only suitable for small events (under 30 people)

## Budget Planning & Cost Breakdown

### Budget Tiers for a 50-Person Event

| Category | Budget | Mid-Range | Luxury |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catering | $50/person | $100/person | $200/person |
| Staff (service, setup) | $20/person | $40/person | $80/person |
| Venue (villa/pavilion) | $500–2,000 | $2,000–5,000 | $5,000+ |
| Coordinator | $300 | $600 | $1,200 |
| Décor / Flowers | $300 | $1,000 | $3,000+ |
| Music / DJ | $200 | $500 | $1,500+ |
| Photography (4–6h) | $300 | $800 | $2,000+ |
| **Total** | **$3,550–4,550** | **$6,900–8,900** | **$13,830+** |

### Money-Saving Tips

1. **Daytime events cost less** (lunch cheaper than dinner; tea cheaper than cocktails)
2. **Buffet or BBQ < plated service** (lower staffing costs)
3. **Single menu option < multiple choices** (easier prep, less waste)
4. **Local ingredients < imported** (source from morning market)
5. **Weekday < weekend** (lower vendor availability premiums)
6. **Group catering < à la carte pricing** (economies of scale)
