### H2: Types of Corporate Retreats & Catering Needs

**Word Count:** 400 words

**Sales/Revenue Team Retreats**
- Goal: Motivate, celebrate achievements, build camaraderie
- Meals: Energizing breakfasts, impressive lunches, celebratory dinners
- Vibe: Upscale but not stuffy
- Example Menu: Fresh acai bowls at breakfast, Thai cuisine for lunch (shared, interactive), fine dining for final dinner

**Engineering/Product Retreats**
- Goal: Problem-solve, brainstorm, foster innovation
- Meals: Efficient (not time-consuming), healthy options, casual environment
- Vibe: Relaxed, focused on the work not the dining
- Example Menu: DIY breakfast stations, salad + grain bowls for lunch (quick), casual BBQ for final night

**Cross-Functional / Whole Company Retreats**
- Goal: Unite silos, celebrate diversity, strengthen culture
- Meals: Inclusive (accommodate all diets), celebratory, showcase local culture
- Vibe: Formal day 1-2, increasingly casual
- Example Menu: Day 1 Welcome: Balinese welcome ceremony + light appetizers; Day 2 Team Lunches: Variety stations; Day 3 Celebration: Fine dining with company CEO remarks

**Board / Executive Retreats**
- Goal: Strategic planning, decision-making, relationship building
- Meals: Premium, impressive, unhurried timing
- Vibe: Sophisticated, professional but personable
- Example Menu: Continental breakfast with briefing, multi-course lunches with conversation space, curated wine dinner

**Leadership Development / Coaching Retreats**
- Goal: Personal growth, team development, vulnerable conversations
- Meals: Nourishing, healthy, time for informal bonding
- Vibe: Supportive, low-pressure dining
- Example Menu: Plant-forward breakfasts, balanced lunches, evening gatherings around fire with light snacks

### H2: Multi-Day Retreat Menu Planning

**Word Count:** 500 words

**Breakfast**

### H2: Dietary Needs at Corporate Retreats

**Word Count:** 350 words

**The Corporate Complexity**
- 30-100 person retreats = high dietary diversity
- International teams = cultural dietary preferences
- Wellness-focused companies = vegan/vegetarian emphasis
- Health-conscious executives = low-carb/paleo requests

**Pre-Retreat Survey Process**
1. Company sends dietary form to all attendees
2. Collect: allergies, vegetarian/vegan, health goals, cultural preferences
3. Chef analyzes data (creates accommodation plan)
4. Company confirms final headcount with dietary breakdown
5. Chef communicates back: "Here's how I'll handle each dietary need"

**Example: 60-Person Retreat Analysis**
- 8 vegetarian/vegan
- 5 gluten-free
- 3 seafood allergies
- 4 "health-conscious" (low-carb preference)
- 40 omnivore with no restrictions

**Chef Solution:**
- All meals have vegetarian/vegan equivalent (not labeled, equal quality)
- Gluten-free prep area to prevent cross-contamination
- Seafood served separately; non-seafood protein for those 3 people
- Grain/starch options + low-carb vegetable options at every meal
- Everything labeled so allergic individuals know what's safe

**Wellness Integration**
- Organic ingredients when possible (premium companies expect this)
- Hydration stations throughout day
- Healthy snack options emphasized
- Indulgent meals balanced with light meals
- Alcohol optional (never assumed)

### H2: Budget & Logistics

**Word Count:** 350 words

**Cost Per Person (3-day retreat)**

| Service Level | Cost/Person | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic Catering** | $50-70/day | 3 meals, standard prep, no customization |
| **Mid-Range Private Chef** | $70-100/day | 3 meals, some dietary accommodation, better ingredients |
| **Premium/Fine Dining** | $100-150+/day | 3 meals, full dietary customization, chef interaction, premium sourcing |

**Example: 60-Person, 3-Day Retreat at $80/person/day**
- Total cost: $14,400
- Food: $3,600/day ($60/person)
- Chef labor: $1,200/day ($20/person)
- Service/logistics: $800/day ($13/person)
- Breakdown: $50 food, $20 labor, $10 logistics

**Logistics Coordination**
- Kitchen space: Venue kitchen or catering space rented
- Meal timing: Align with work schedule (when do morning sessions end?)
- Service style: Family-style (build connection) or plated (formal)
- Dietary tracking: Master spreadsheet of accommodations
- Staff: Chef + 1-2 assistants minimum for 60-person retreat

**Money-Saving Tips**
- Fewer plated courses (family-style sharing is cheaper + team-building)
- Embrace Bali ingredients (seasonal, local, cheaper)
- Limited dietary options (vs. custom for each person — be realistic)
- Multi-use ingredients (same proteins across multiple meals, adjusted prep)

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**Word Count:** 250 words

**Q: Can a private chef cater 100+ people?**
A: Typical max is 75-100 with assistants. Larger retreats need catering company infrastructure.

**Q: What if team has very diverse dietary needs?**
A: Strong private chef handles this. Survey in advance, create accommodation plan, communicate clearly at service.

**Q: Should meals be formal or casual?**
A: Mix both. Formal Day 1 (signal quality), casual Days 2-3 (team bonding). Balance is key.

**Q: Can the chef prepare specific cuisines?**
A: Yes, that's part of their job. Thai, Mediterranean, Balinese, Asian fusion, international comfort food. Discuss in advance.

**Q: What if someone has a food allergy we didn't know about?**
A: Professional chef can improvise safely. Communicate immediately upon discovery.

**Q: How do meals encourage team bonding?**
A: Family-style service, chef interaction, diverse seating arrangements, sharing dishes.

**Q: Should the chef attend meals?**
A: Optional but valuable. Brief explanation of dishes builds connection and shows care.
