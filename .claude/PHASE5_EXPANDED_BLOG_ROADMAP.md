**Every post must include:**
- ✅ Keyword-rich title (primary keyword in first 5 words)

- ✅ H1 with primary keyword
- ✅ H2s with keyword variations (8-12 H2s per post)
- ✅ Internal links (6-10 strategic links per post)
- ✅ Multimedia (8-15 images with semantic alt text)
- ✅ CTA (natural, not pushy)
- ✅ FAQ section (8-15 questions)
- ✅ Testimonial or case study (where applicable)
- ✅ Schema markup (Article, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage)
- ✅ Word count: 1,800-4,000 words (depending on tier)
- ✅ Readability: Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences), subheadings every 300 words

**Before Publishing:**
- ✅ Spell & grammar check (Grammarly)

- ✅ Keyword density check (1-2% primary keyword)
