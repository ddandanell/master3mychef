## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

**Risk: Keywords not ranking after optimization**
- Mitigation: Start with Tier 1 opportunities (QWS 70+), quick wins first
- Monitor: Weekly ranking checks, adjust within 2 weeks if no movement

**Risk: Blog traffic is minimal**
- Mitigation: Ensure internal linking (8-10 links per post to service pages)
- Ensure keywords naturally integrated (not over-optimized)
- Promote via email, social, team updates

**Risk: Mobile issues causing ranking penalty**
- Mitigation: Test all pages on mobile during Week 1 (May 25-31)
- Fix any show-stoppers before launching new content (June 1)

**Risk: Team capacity (writing 10+ blog posts in 3 weeks)**
- Mitigation: Parallel workflows (SEO optimizes pages while Blog writes)
- If needed, reduce to 8 posts instead of 10, add 2 in Phase 6
