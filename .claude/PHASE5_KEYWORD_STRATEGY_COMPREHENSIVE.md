### Phase 2: Opportunity Ranking (May 27-31)

**Scoring System: Quick Win Score (QWS)**
Each keyword gets a score 0-100 based on:
```
QWS = (Volume × 0.2) + (Intent Score × 0.3) + (Gap Score × 0.3) + (Ease Score × 0.2)

Volume: Normalize 0-50
  - 0-100 searches = 10 points
  - 100-500 = 20 points
  - 500-1000 = 30 points
  - 1000+ = 50 points

Intent Score: 0-50
  - Informational only = 10 points
  - Mixed (some commercial) = 25 points
  - High commercial = 50 points

Gap Score: 0-50 (what competitors have vs. us)
  - Competitors rank 1-3 for it, we don't = 50 points
  - Competitors rank 5-10, we're absent = 40 points
  - Mixed presence = 25 points
  - We own it = 0 points (already optimized)

Ease Score: 0-30 (ease to improve)
  - Currently at 40-60 position = 30 points
  - Currently at 60-80 = 25 points
  - Currently at 80+ = 20 points
  - Currently 20-40 = 10 points (near top, harder to improve)
```

**Tier 1 Opportunities (QWS 70-100):**
- High traffic, moderate competition
- Commercial intent keywords
- Can move from 40-50 → 15-25 with solid optimization
- Focus: 8-12 of these

**Tier 2 Opportunities (QWS 50-70):**
- Medium traffic or high competition
- Informational with commercial intent
- Can move from 50-70 → 20-40
- Focus: 8-12 of these

**Tier 3 Opportunities (QWS 30-50):**
- Long-term plays, low current traffic
- Build domain authority over time
- Secondary blog topics
- Focus: 4-8 of these

## 🔗 Internal Linking Strategy (Cluster Model)

**Hub-and-Spoke Architecture:**

**Hub:** `/` (Homepage)
- Link to: `/fine-dining`, `/catering`, `/events`, `/in-villa-service`, `/locations`
- Anchor: Primary keywords (fine dining, catering, events, villa service)

**Primary Cluster: Fine Dining**
- Hub: `/fine-dining`
- Spokes: Blog posts on chef hiring, private chef cost, fine dining experiences, menu planning
- Internal links: 5-7 links per blog post back to `/fine-dining`

**Secondary Cluster: Events & Catering**
- Hub: `/events`
- Spokes: Destination wedding guide, corporate retreat, luxury event planning, intimate dinners
- Cross-link: 3-4 links from event posts to `/catering`, from catering to `/events`

**Authority Cluster: Education**
- Hub: Blog section
- Spokes: All educational content (what's private chef, training, costs)
- Purpose: Build domain authority, drive organic traffic
- Link back: Each blog post links 2-3 times to service pages

**Location Cluster: Regional**
- Hub: `/locations`
- Spokes: Location-specific blog content (Ubud dining, Seminyak catering, Canggu options)
- Purpose: Capture location-specific search traffic
- Link pattern: Blog posts link to locations page, locations page links back to blog
