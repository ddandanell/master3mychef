## 📊 How to Use This Template

1. **Copy this file** at the end of each week (Friday EOD):
   - `PHASE5_WEEKLY_REPORT_2026-05-25_WEEK1.md`
   - `PHASE5_WEEKLY_REPORT_2026-06-01_WEEK2.md`
   - `PHASE5_WEEKLY_REPORT_2026-06-08_WEEK3.md`
   - `PHASE5_WEEKLY_REPORT_2026-06-15_WEEK4.md`

2. **Fill in sections as work completes:**
   - Completion Status: Update daily as pages finish
   - Technical Verification: Friday (after final testing)
   - SEO Impact: Friday (pull GSC data, record metrics)
   - Effort Tracking: Friday (total hours for the week)

3. **Archive reports:**
   - Save all 4 weekly reports in `reports/` folder
   - Use them to assess Phase 5 impact on June 22 (Phase 6 kickoff)

4. **Red flags to watch:**
   - Pages slipping more than 2 hours per day (effort bloat)
   - CWV degradation (> 10% slower LCP)
   - Keyword drops (>5 position decline in top 30 keywords)
   - Multiple 404s from internal link additions
   - Blog posts not indexing within 7–10 days
