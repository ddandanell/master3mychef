## 🎯 Phase 6 Overview

After Phase 5 content optimization (May 25 – June 21) establishes on-page SEO foundation, Phase 6 focuses on building topical authority and domain authority through strategic link acquisition across four pillars:

1. **Local Bali hospitality networks** (industry partnerships, villa guides)
2. **Niche directories** (fine dining, catering, event services in Southeast Asia)
3. **Travel & lifestyle platforms** (Bali travel guides, luxury hospitality reviews)
4. **Press & media outreach** (Bali tourism blogs, hospitality publications)

**Why Phase 6 matters:** Content optimization moves keywords to position 20–30. Links push them into top 10–15, where conversion significantly increases. Backlinks also accelerate crawl frequency and indexation for new blog posts.

| Risk | Mitigation |

| Links take 3+ months to appear in GSC | Expected; monitor Link Report in GSC; expect lag of 6–12 weeks |
