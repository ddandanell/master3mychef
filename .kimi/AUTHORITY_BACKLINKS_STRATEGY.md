## Authority estimate note
**Authority estimate = heuristic range** based on brand visibility, search footprint, editorial depth, and category reputation. Validate with Ahrefs/Moz before paying for any placement.

### Range guide
- **75-85** = major regional publisher / large marketplace
- **60-74** = established multi-market travel or wedding brand
- **45-59** = strong niche Bali publisher or villa platform
- **30-44** = solid local niche site or specialist directory
- **20-29** = smaller boutique partner target

## Partnership value propositions

### For villa rental partners
**Offer structure**
- Referral commission or fixed preferred-partner fee
- Net partner rates on repeat villa bookings
- Priority response on same-day or next-day requests

**Operational value**
- Concierge-friendly booking handoff
- One team for chef, service staff, setup, cleanup
- Menu formats from family dinner to formal celebration
- Clear quotes. Clear timing. No hidden coordination burden.

**Commercial value**
- More guest satisfaction without adding internal workload
- Upsell opportunity on arrival night, birthdays, BBQs, and breakfast
- Better review potential because food becomes part of the villa experience

### For wedding planners
**Offer structure**
- Planner referral fee or preferred-partner terms
- Fast proposal turnaround for live quotes
- Tasting and revision process for couple approvals

**Operational value**
- Menu flexibility across plated, buffet, family-style, cocktail, and brunch
- Strong dietary handling for international guest lists
- Reliable load-in, service timing, and reset discipline
- One point of contact for changes and guest-count updates

**Planner-facing promise**
- Fewer day-of surprises
- Stronger kitchen communication
- A catering partner who understands Bali villa logistics

### For retreat organizers
**Offer structure**
- Multi-day pricing with repeat-service efficiencies
- Fixed meal-plan packages by headcount and length of stay
- Optional grocery, snack, and hydration support

**Operational value**
- Dietary customization without menu sprawl
- Quiet early-morning setup and discreet service
- Multi-day consistency across breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
- Health-forward menus without sacrificing satisfaction

**Organizer-facing promise**
- Less host stress
- Better guest energy across the schedule
- Clear meal planning from day 1 to departure

## Implementation plan

### Week 1 — Asset pack + first 10 pitches
1. Prepare partner asset pack:
   - 80-word company intro
   - 3 menu PDFs or links
   - 6 best food/event photos
   - 3 case studies: villa dinner, wedding, retreat
   - WhatsApp contact and fast-response promise
2. Submit / pitch top 10 immediate targets.
3. Start with the fastest wins:
   - Bridestory
   - WedSetter
   - Bali Wedding Association
   - BaliSpirit
4. Send editorial pitches to:
   - Honeycombers
   - The Asia Collective
   - Bali Buddies

### Week 2 — Follow-up + second wave
1. Follow up all Week 1 pitches after 5 business days.
2. Pitch second-wave villa partners:
   - Elite Havens
   - Ministry of Villas
   - The Luxe Nomad
   - The Bali Bible
3. Offer one finished outline, not just a vague idea.
4. Push for partner-page inclusion if editorial is slow.

### Week 3 — Draft and ship content
1. Write 2 guest posts from the accepted topics.
2. Supply unique photos and a short founder bio.
3. Ask for anchor text that stays natural:
   - myCHEF Bali private chef
   - Bali wedding catering by myCHEF
   - Bali retreat catering by myCHEF
4. Secure at least 1 villa partner referral page.

### Week 4 — Close links and measure
1. Check live status of all won links.
2. Add live URLs to the tracker.
3. Re-pitch non-responders with a tighter angle.
4. Turn successful guest posts into new proof for the next outreach batch.
