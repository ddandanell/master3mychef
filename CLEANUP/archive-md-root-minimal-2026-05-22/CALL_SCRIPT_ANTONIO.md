## CALL SCRIPT

### OPENING (30 seconds)
```
"Hi Antonio, David from myCHEF. Great to connect with you.
We'd like to have you do a test dinner for us next week 
so we can evaluate a potential partnership. Got a few minutes?"
```

---

### QUESTION 1: Availability (1 min)
```
"Perfect. Here's what I'm thinking — we'd love to do a test dinner 
between May 20-22 at one of our partner villas.

Are you available any of those dates?
- May 20th?
- May 21st?
- May 22nd?

Which works best for you?"
```

**Listen for**: Specific dates confirmed

**If none of those dates work**:
```
"When would work? We need it done before the 23rd for partnership decision."
```

---

### QUESTION 2: Menu Approach (1 min)
```
"Now — for the dinner itself, here's how I see it:

Option A: You do your signature menu — show us your best work
Option B: You use our template to show how you execute our style

Which would you prefer?"
```

**Listen for**: His preference + confidence level

**If hesitant**:
```
"What would make you most comfortable showcasing your skills?"
```

---

### QUESTION 3: Location & Logistics (1 min)
```
"Location-wise, we'll host it at one of our premium villas.
Questions:
1. Do you need any special kitchen equipment? (We have access to full setups)
2. How many hours will you need for prep + service + cleanup?
3. Can you bring a sous chef or do you work solo?
4. Any dietary restrictions in our evaluation team I should know about?"
```

**Listen for**:
- Equipment needs
- Time required
- Team size
- Special considerations

---

### QUESTION 4: The Evaluation Process (1 min)
```
"Here's how we'll evaluate — we have three people joining:
- Me (David) — partnership fit and business side
- Paco — operations and execution
- Alessandro — culinary quality and technique

We'll score you on:
1. Culinary quality (food taste, technique, presentation)
2. Michelin standard (passion, precision, innovation)
3. Partnership mindset (willingness to collaborate, flexibility)
4. Menu differentiation (what makes you unique vs our current offering)
5. Reliability (professionalism, punctuality, communication)

Each scored 1-10. We want to know: can we partner successfully?

Does that make sense?"
```

---

### CLOSING (30 seconds)
```
"So [SUMMARIZE]:
- Date: [CONFIRMED DATE]
- Menu: [HIS SIGNATURE / OUR TEMPLATE]
- Location: [VILLA, DATE/TIME]
- Duration: [HOURS]
- Team: [SOLO / WITH SOUS]
- Evaluation: [Scorecard explained]

After the dinner, we'll have a decision meeting. 
If we all say GO, we sign a partnership agreement.
If not, we thank you for your time and continue looking.
Sound fair?"
```

**Expected**: "Yes" or acknowledgment
