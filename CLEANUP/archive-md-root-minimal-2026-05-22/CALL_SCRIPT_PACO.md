```

2. How long does menu design take? (Days T-7 to T-?)

### QUESTION 2: Deposit Structure (1.5 min)
```
"Now the financial piece. I need to know:
1. What % deposit do customers pay upfront? (We're thinking 25%)
2. When is that deposit due? (7 days before? 3 days?)
3. What % is due on the day of service?
4. What payment methods? (Stripe? Bank transfer? Cash at villa? WhatsApp Pay?)
5. What's the cancellation refund policy at different dates?

Example: 25% deposit due 7 days before, 75% due 48 hours before, 
full refund if cancelled 14+ days prior. Does that match what you want?"

**Listen for**:

- Final payment timing
