## COPYWRITER BRIEF

**Voice**: Warm, confident, knowledgeable. Like a trusted Bali insider who happens to run a catering company.

**Tone by Page**:
- **Catering pages**: Direct, practical, "we've got this"
- **Events pages**: Emotional, aspirational, "your special day"
- **Corporate page**: Professional, detail-oriented, "we understand procurement"

**Avoid**:
- Generic superlatives ("best in Bali", "unforgettable")
- Passive voice
- Jargon without explanation
- Long paragraphs (max 3 sentences per paragraph)

**Use**:
- Specific numbers ("1 waiter per 10 guests")
- Social proof ("Sofia has coordinated 200+ events")
- Risk reversal ("free cancellation 6h+")
- Urgency without pressure ("peak season books 1–3 months ahead")
