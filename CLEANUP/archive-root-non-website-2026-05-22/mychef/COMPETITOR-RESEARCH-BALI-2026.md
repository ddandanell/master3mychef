## 6. Common Offerings Analysis

### Menu Types (What Everyone Offers)

1. **Canapés / Finger Food** — Universal. Served at welcome receptions.
2. **Buffet (Indonesian / Western / BBQ)** — Standard for mid-large events.
3. **Plated Multi-Course** — Luxury segment. Fine dining positioning.
4. **Family-Style Sharing** — Trending. Boards, bowls, shared platters.
5. **Grazing Tables** — Instagram-driven. Cheese, charcuterie, fruits.
6. **Live Cooking Stations** — Interactive. BBQ, pasta, tacos, satay.
7. **Themed Cuisine Nights** — Mexican, Mediterranean, Japanese, Indian.
8. **Floating Breakfast / Brunch** — Villa-specific Instagram product.

### Service Inclusions (What Most Include)

- Chef + sous chef
- Wait staff (1 per 10 guests typical)
- Basic serving equipment / chafing dishes
- Setup & breakdown
- Kitchen cleanup

### Service Inclusions (What Premium Adds)

- Event director / manager
- Bar & bartenders
- Tableware, linens, glassware
- Floral / basic décor
- AV / sound / lighting
- Photography / videography
- Entertainment coordination

### Dietary Accommodation (Universal)

- Vegetarian
- Vegan
- Gluten-free
- Halal
- Nut allergies
- Kid-friendly

## 7. Positioning Gaps for myCHEF

### What Competitors Do Well

- **Scale**: Bali Catering & Events, Bali Catering Company handle 200+ guests
- **Specialization**: Kaminari (Japanese), Queens (Indian), Island Graze (grazing)
- **Experience**: Bali Good Food (28 years), Dijon (25+ years)
- **Platform**: Take a Chef (marketplace reach)
- **Villa bundles**: Bali Premium Villa, Ministry of Villas (chef included)

### What Competitors Miss (myCHEF Opportunities)

1. **Unified brand across fine dining + catering + events**
   - Most competitors specialize in ONE segment. myCHEF owns all three.

2. **Same-day WhatsApp confirmation**
   - Most require 1–2 weeks minimum. myCHEF's speed is a differentiator.

3. **Transparent grocery pass-through (no markup)**
   - Competitors typically mark up ingredients 10–25%. myCHEF's transparency is unique.

4. **Michelin-trained leadership + Indonesian team**
   - M&M has Michelin chef but is catering-only. myCHEF combines fine dining credibility with local scale.

5. **Certified partner programme for villas**
   - No competitor has a formal villa partner certification programme.

6. **Full-stack events (catering + bar + décor + AV + staffing)**
   - Only Bali Catering & Events and Bali Catering Company come close, but they lack the fine dining brand tier.

7. **Pricing calculator / instant quote funnel**
   - No competitor offers a self-service quote tool. All require enquiry → wait for proposal.

8. **Area-specific SEO landing pages**
   - myCHEF has 15+ area pages. Competitors typically have 1 generic page.

9. **Structured data & AI search optimization**
   - No competitor has comprehensive JSON-LD across all pages.

10. **Recovery brunch as standard add-on**
    - Mentioned by Bali Good Food but not standardized. myCHEF can own "the morning after."
