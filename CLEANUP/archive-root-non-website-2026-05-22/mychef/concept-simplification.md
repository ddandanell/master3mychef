## Brand-Level Concept Simplification

### 1. One-Sentence Explanation
A private chef company in Bali that cooks for you, whether you want one fancy dinner, a chef for your whole vacation, or a full catered event.

### 2. Super Simple Version
We send great chefs to your villa. Three ways: fancy dinner, daily chef, or big party.

### 3. Sharper Business Version
A three-service hospitality company. Fine dining for luxury guests. Daily chefs for villa renters. Full-service catering for events. One brand, three completely different offers.

### 4. Customer-Friendly Version
Three ways to eat well in Bali. Want a once-in-a-lifetime dinner? We do that. Want a chef to cook your family meals every day? We do that. Throwing a wedding or party? We do that too.

### 5. Short Headline (3-8 words)
Private chefs for every moment in Bali

### 6. Alternative Headlines
- Great food, your villa, zero effort
- A chef for every occasion in Bali
- From intimate dinners to full events
- Bali's private kitchen, three ways
- You relax. We cook.

### 7. Graphic Idea
A split-screen image: left side shows a couple at a candlelit dinner table, center shows a family at a breakfast table, right side shows a lively party table. Same villa setting, same warm lighting, three different moods. One line at the bottom: "Your villa. Our chefs. Any occasion."

An overhead drone shot of a long dining table set for dinner in a tropical villa at golden hour. The table runs from lush garden on one end to the infinity pool on the other. Candles lit. Plates arranged. The table tells the story: some seats intimate, some family-style, some banquet. Warm light, no faces, just the promise.

It is like having three restaurants in one building. Upstairs is the Michelin-star tasting room. Downstairs is the family kitchen where someone cooks your daily meals. Out back is the event hall for parties. Same building. Same quality. Completely different experiences. You just pick the door.

### 10. Final Cleaned Version
We are the people who cook for you in Bali. Three choices. One great dinner. A chef every day. Or a full team for your event. Pick what fits. We handle the rest.

## DESIGN NOTES

### Tone of Voice
- Short sentences. Short words.
- Active voice always.
- No "curated experiences" or "bespoke journeys" or "elevated dining."
- No "immerse yourself" or "indulge in" or "savor the moment."
- Write like you are explaining it to a friend over coffee.
- Let the luxury speak through specifics, not adjectives.

### What to Avoid
- Do not mix the three services on one page. They are different products for different people.
- Do not use the same design language for all three. Fine dining is dark and intimate. Catering is bright and casual. Events are energetic and alive.
- Do not over-explain. The visitor already wants a private chef. Just tell them which kind.

### What to Emphasize
- For Fine Dining: the once-in-a-lifetime feeling. The intimacy. The craft.
- For Catering: the ease. The freedom from daily cooking. The vacation feeling.
- For Events: the full service. The fact that you handle everything. The peace of mind.
