## Detailed Feedback

### What Worked Well

1. **The Villa Catering page pricing tiers are actually perfect for my use case.** The "Weekly Villa Chef" at IDR 28M/week for 3 meals/day with custom menu planning and daily grocery shopping is almost exactly what I need for my 7-day retreat. This page gave me the most concrete information to plug into my spreadsheet.

2. **Multiple contact channels.** WhatsApp (promised 1-hour response), email, and phone. As someone remote in London, having WhatsApp as a primary channel is exactly what I need for async communication across time zones.

3. **The "Retreat" event type exists.** It's in the dropdown on both the Contact page and the Events form. Someone at least thought to include it.

4. **5-step planning process is clear and visual.** The numbered circles with connecting lines communicate a professional, structured approach. "Custom Proposal" and "Event Coordination" steps suggest a thorough handover.

5. **Dietary restriction FAQ is reassuring.** The explicit mention of "vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, and more" with "our chefs will craft a menu that works for everyone" addresses one of my core concerns.

6. **"Replacement chef coverage for days off" on Villa Catering.** This operational detail shows they've thought about continuity — something I worry about for a 7-day event.

7. **8 years in Bali / 500+ villas served.** These stats, if true, suggest an established operation.

### What Was Missing

1. **No retreat-specific landing page or content.** Despite listing "Retreats" as an event type, there is ZERO dedicated retreat content. No page explaining how they handle multi-day retreats. No mention of Ubud specifically (Bali's wellness capital). No discussion of:
   - Daily menu rotation to avoid repetition over 7 days
   - Ayurvedic / wellness / organic menu options
   - Post-yoga meal timing and nutrition
   - Group dietary restriction management systems
   - Retreat-specific staffing (e.g., enough servers for 25 guests)

2. **No food safety or hygiene information ANYWHERE.** For someone whose previous retreat had a food poisoning incident, this is the #1 thing I need to see. No mention of:
   - Food safety certifications (HACCP, food handler licenses)
   - Kitchen hygiene protocols
   - Cold chain management in Bali's tropical climate
   - Fresh ingredient sourcing standards
   - Food temperature monitoring
   - Liability insurance

3. **No retreat testimonials or case studies.** The only testimonial on the entire Events page is a wedding (Sarah & Michael). I need to see testimonials from retreat organizers — preferably wellness retreats — confirming reliability over multi-day events.

4. **No chef profiles or credentials.** "Michelin-trained" is mentioned but I see no chef photos, bios, training backgrounds, or specialization areas. Who would actually be cooking for my retreat? A mystery.

5. **No tasting option or sample menus.** For a IDR 40-60M commitment from London, I need some way to validate quality before booking. No mention of tasting sessions, sample menu PDFs, or video of food preparation.

6. **Gallery is broken.** 5 of 6 gallery images don't load. Several event type card images are also broken. This is either a broken site or a template with placeholder images. Either way, it destroys trust.

7. **No discussion of cost structure for retreats.** The Villa Catering page shows IDR 28M/week for chef time (groceries separate), and the Events page says "Custom quoted." But there's no framework for understanding what a 7-day retreat for 25 people might actually cost. I need transparency, not "contact us for a quote."

8. **No multi-day event duration field in forms.** The Events form asks for Event Date (singular) but not duration. For a 7-day retreat, this is a critical omission. I'd have to put it in the free text field.

9. **Phone number appears to be a placeholder.** "+62 812 3456 7890" is suspiciously round. Combined with the Kimi badge on every page, this strongly suggests the site is AI-generated and not a real operational business yet.

10. **No cancellation policy or contract terms visible.** The FAQ mentions "What is your cancellation policy?" but I couldn't see the answer. For a large booking, I need to know the terms upfront.

### What Was Confusing or Frustrating

1. **The "Explore" button on the homepage hero does nothing (or it's unclear what it does).** I clicked it and it just seemed to scroll slightly. No clear CTA for retreat planners.

2. **Villa Catering vs. Events Services — which do I choose?** My 7-day retreat with 25 guests sits in a gray area. Villa Catering has the weekly pricing I need, but Events Services mentions retreats and has the staffing/rentals add-ons. The site doesn't clarify which service is appropriate for multi-day group retreats. I shouldn't have to guess.

3. **Broken images everywhere.** On the Events page, the Retreats card image is broken, Corporate Events image is broken, and 5 of 6 gallery images are broken. For a premium catering brand, this is unacceptable. It suggests either neglect or that the site isn't live yet.

4. **The Events form doesn't have a "Duration" field.** I can specify "Retreat" as event type and "21-50" guests, but there's nowhere to say "7 days." This seems like a basic oversight for an events company.

5. **"Kimi Agent" badge on every page.** This undermines the premium positioning. It makes the site feel like a template or AI-generated experiment rather than a real, established catering business. Combined with the placeholder phone number, my trust is eroding.

6. **Budget dropdown doesn't align with my range well.** My budget is IDR 40-60M. The options are "Under 50M" or "50-100M." I span both categories, so neither feels right. A 7-day retreat for 25 at IDR 40M total would be IDR ~230K per person per day for all meals — is that realistic?

7. **No information about what's included vs. excluded.** The Weekly Villa Chef says "Chef time + groceries billed separately" but the Events form has checkboxes for Private Chef, Bartenders, Waiters, Glassware, etc. Do I need both? What's the total cost picture?

### Would I Book for My Retreat?

**Not yet. Here's why:**

The site has promise — the Villa Catering weekly plan pricing is competitive, the planning process looks professional, and they clearly know the Bali villa market. But as someone who experienced a food poisoning incident at a previous retreat, I have a very high bar for trust signals, and this site doesn't clear it.

My specific blockers:
1. **No food safety information** — This is non-negotiable for me after my previous incident. I need to see certifications, protocols, or at least explicit commitments to food safety standards.
2. **Broken gallery and placeholder phone number** — These make me question whether this is a real, operational business or a website prototype. I'm not risking my 25-guest retreat on an unproven vendor.
3. **No retreat-specific social proof** — I need testimonials from retreat organizers, ideally wellness retreat planners in Ubud, confirming consistent quality over multi-day events.
4. **The Kimi badge screams "template"** — It undermines every premium design choice on the site. I can't take a business seriously if their own website advertises the platform it was built on.

That said, the Weekly Villa Chef pricing (IDR 28M/week + groceries) fits my budget range well. If I could verify this is a real business with real operational experience, I'd be inclined to start a WhatsApp conversation. But from the website alone, I don't have enough confidence to reach out yet.

### The One Thing That Would Most Improve My Confidence

**Add a dedicated "Retreats & Wellness Events" page** with:
- At least 2-3 retreat-specific testimonials (with organizer names, retreat type, guest count, and location)
- A retreat case study (e.g., "7-Day Wellness Retreat for 20 Guests in Ubud — How We Did It")
- Clear food safety protocols and any certifications
- Sample retreat menu (breakfast, lunch, dinner for one day)
- Photos of actual retreat catering (not generic stock images)
- Transparent pricing framework for multi-day retreats
- A note about chef backup/rotation for multi-day events

If that page existed and was credible, I'd go from "not ready to reach out" to "filling out the WhatsApp inquiry within 10 minutes."
