## Top 10 Issues (Prioritized by Impact)

### 🔴 Critical (Must Fix)

**1. Gallery Images Broken (5-6 of 6 on Events page, 11+ on Catering)**
- Impact: Destroys trust for all persona types
- Fix: Regenerate or replace broken image assets

**2. Chef Has No Name**
- Impact: "Our head chef" feels impersonal for a luxury service
- Fix: Add a real or representative chef name, photo, and personal story

**3. No Family Testimonials**
- Impact: Families (Chen, Larsens) have no social proof they can relate to
- Fix: Add 2-3 family/multi-gen testimonials

**4. No Corporate Content**
- Impact: David (corporate planner) scored lowest at 4.5/10
- Fix: Add corporate event section with testimonials, case study, safety certs

### 🟡 High Priority

**5. "Groceries Billed Separately" Creates Budget Fear**
- Impact: Chen Family and Larsens both flagged this as a dealbreaker
- Fix: Add grocery cost estimates or all-inclusive package options

**6. No Retreat-Specific Content**
- Impact: Emma scored 5.8/10, needs wellness/retreat evidence
- Fix: Add retreat testimonials, wellness menu samples, food safety info

**7. Tone Too Fancy for Casual Groups**
- Impact: Jake (bachelor party) felt intimidated by "Michelin-level" language
- Fix: Add a dedicated "Group Dining & BBQ" page with casual tone

**8. No Multi-Cultural Cuisine Evidence**
- Impact: Priya & Raj (Indian wedding) — critical gap for a huge market
- Fix: Add multi-cultural wedding page with fusion menu samples

**9. Inconsistent Contact Experience**
- Impact: Two different forms (Catering page vs Contact page) confuse users
- Fix: Unify to one consistent form experience

**10. No All-Inclusive Pricing Calculator**
- Impact: Larsens couldn't calculate total cost for 14 people
- Fix: Add simple cost calculator: guests x days x package = estimate

## Top 10 Strengths

1. **Fine Dining page is stunning** — Dark luxury aesthetic with gold accents scored 8-9/10 across all personas
2. **WhatsApp integration** — Universally praised as the right choice for Bali market
3. **Menu presentation** — 7-course structure with transparent pricing is genuinely impressive
4. **5-step planning process** — Events page timeline builds confidence
5. **Trust signal counters** — "500+ villas, 12,000+ guests, 4.9 rating" powerful social proof
6. **Three distinct visual identities** — Homepage gateway with 3 different card styles works well
7. **Responsive mobile design** — Works well on phone (primary traffic source)
8. **FAQ quality** — Comprehensive, well-written answers across all pages
9. **Dynamic contact form** — Service-specific conditional fields are smart
10. **Food photography** — Hero images and menu shots are world-class
