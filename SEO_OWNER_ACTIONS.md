# SEO — Owner Action List (the non-code levers)

_Last updated: 2026-07-01. The technical blockers are fixed in code (prerendering,
internal linking, sitemap, redirects, broken-link cleanup). What's left moves the
needle far more than any code change — and only you can do it._

## Why this matters (the one-paragraph truth)
As of the latest GSC data, only **55 of ~222 pages are indexed (26%)**. The dominant
cause (empty pages to Google) is now **fixed**. The next dominant cause is **authority**:
the site has **≈1 backlink**, so Google won't spend crawl budget indexing 200+ pages.
No technical fix changes that. The three actions below are what get the other ~150
pages crawled and indexed.

- [ ] Turn on messaging; add the WhatsApp link.
- [ ] Get **5–10 real reviews** from past clients (message happy customers a direct review link). Reviews = ranking + trust.
- [ ] Post weekly updates (a dish, an event) — signals an active profile.

## 🔴 P0 — First 15–30 real backlinks (over the next 4–8 weeks)
Quality > quantity. One link from an established Bali travel/villa site beats 50 you build
yourself. Target, in rough priority:

- [ ] **Villa & property managers** you already work with — ask for a "recommended chef" link on their site.
- [ ] **Bali directories / listings:** TripAdvisor, The Bali Bible, Honeycombers Bali, Bali tourism directories, Balicheapesttickets/Bali-activities style sites.
- [ ] **Wedding/event platforms:** Bridestory, Hello Bali, local wedding-planner sites (you cater weddings — get listed).
- [ ] **Suppliers & partners:** butchers, wine importers, equipment rental, florists — reciprocal "partners we work with" links.
- [ ] **Digital PR:** email authors of "best private chef in Bali" / "best villa catering" roundup articles asking to be included (offer a photo + short blurb).
- [ ] **Your own profiles (do all):** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube — each linking mychef.id. These are baseline citations.
- [ ] **Local citations:** Yelp, Foursquare, Indonesian business directories, expat-in-Bali forums/Facebook groups (genuine participation, not spam).

**Do NOT:** buy links, build a network of your own "food blog" domains (PBN), or mass-submit
to junk directories. Google ignores or penalizes these — see the note below.

## The PBN question (asked & answered)
Buying 10 domains, writing food content, and linking them to mychef.id **does not work**:
new domains have ~zero authority to pass, Google detects self-built networks easily, and
it risks a link-scheme penalty. Your one existing backlink
(`villa-catering-bali.online`) is exactly this pattern and it's doing nothing. Put that
time into GBP + real partner links instead.
