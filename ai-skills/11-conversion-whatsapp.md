# 11 — Conversion (WhatsApp)

The primary conversion is WhatsApp via `src/lib/whatsapp.ts`. Every commercial page needs an above-the-fold CTA and a closing CTA. Mobile sticky CTA must stay tappable (≥44px) and not covered by popups.

Bar-services and contact forms use `/api/send-email`, not WhatsApp. Do not invent prices in UI that conflict with `mychef-ai-agent/knowledge-base/KB-01-services-and-pricing.md` without an owner ruling.
