## 3. Case Studies

### Case Study A: The High-End Villa Birthday (Seminyak)
- **Guests:** 12 adults, 4 children.
- **Format:** Poolside Seafood BBQ followed by an 8-course Mediterranean Tasting Menu.
- **Challenge:** Limited kitchen space in a traditional 4-bedroom villa.
- **Solution:** myCHEF deployed a mobile prep station and two additional service staff. We managed a split-service flow where children dined early, followed by a formal seated dinner for adults.
- **Result:** 5-star review highlighting the kitchen reset (left cleaner than found).

### Case Study B: The Destination Wedding Welcome (Uluwatu)
- **Guests:** 65 international guests.
- **Format:** "Balinese Market" Buffet with live suckling pig (Babi Guling) carving station.
- **Challenge:** Clifftop wind and narrow access for catering trucks.
- **Solution:** Used specialized wind-shielded heating equipment and a smaller 1-tonne shuttle for load-in.
- **Result:** Seamless integration with the wedding planning team; zero power interruptions due to dedicated generator management.

### Case Study C: The Executive Yoga Retreat (Ubud)
- **Duration:** 5 days, full-board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks).
- **Guests:** 20 guests, 100% vegan and gluten-free requirement.
- **Challenge:** Maintaining energy levels for intensive physical practice without menu fatigue.
- **Solution:** Developed a rotating "Nutritional Integrity" menu using locally sourced superfoods and Balinese aromatics. Focus on protein-dense plant bases.
- **Result:** Retreat organizer booked myCHEF for 3 subsequent retreats in 2026.
