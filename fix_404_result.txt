## Root Cause (Issues 1 & 3)
The SPA has a two-layer routing system:
1. React Router routes (client-side, in src/App.tsx)
2. inject-meta.ts static pre-render (generates dist/<path>/index.html from BLOG_POSTS in src/data/sitemap.ts)

When a URL is in App.tsx but NOT in BLOG_POSTS/sitemap.ts, client-side navigation works fine,
but direct URL access (used by Google Search Console, social sharing, and link crawlers) hits
Vercel's file system, finds no index.html, and returns 404.

## What Was Wrong

### Issue 1: 3 blog pages returning 404 in production
- /blog/chef-hiring-guide — route existed in App.tsx (line 345), but NO entry in BLOG_POSTS
- /blog/event-planning-bali — route existed in App.tsx (line 346), but NO entry in BLOG_POSTS
- /blog/bali-catering-menu — route existed in App.tsx (line 347), but NO entry in BLOG_POSTS

These three pages are heavily linked from other blog pages and BlogPage.tsx,
but inject-meta.ts never generated dist/blog/<slug>/index.html for them.
