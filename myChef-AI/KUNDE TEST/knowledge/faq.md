### What does "++" mean in a price?
"++" adds **10% service charge and 11% government tax**. Example: IDR 3,500,000++ is
approximately **IDR 4,165,000** all-in per person. (Source: chef's-table page.)

### Is wine pairing included?
No — it's **optional**. Tasting Menu wine pairing is +IDR 850,000/guest; Chef's Table is
+IDR 1,100,000/guest (6–7 glasses, matched by course). If you prefer to bring your own wine,
we open and serve it, and can recommend bottles to source.

### Can I talk directly to the chef?
To keep quality and planning consistent, all communication goes through our office team.
Once everything is confirmed, we coordinate directly with the chef on your behalf. *(See
`learning/customer_patterns.md` Pattern #42 — this is a high-conversion answer.)*

### Do you charge travel fees?
Outside Seminyak/Canggu, travel is **IDR 250,000–700,000** depending on distance and event
size, confirmed before deposit.

### What's the minimum for a tasting menu / chef's table?
Tasting Menu: **4 guests minimum.** Chef's Table: **6 seats maximum, Fri & Sat only.**

### What's the minimum for buffet catering?
**30 guests minimum.** Chef and staff are included.

### Which areas do you cover?
Bali-wide, including Seminyak, Canggu, Ubud, Uluwatu, Sanur, Nusa Dua, Jimbaran, Berawa,
Pererenan, and the Bukit Peninsula.

### Do you handle dietary restrictions / allergies?
Yes — menus can be adapted (e.g. full gluten-free adaptation on BBQ at +IDR 25,000/child).
For serious allergies we confirm specifics with the office before booking.

### How do I book? / Is a deposit required?
We hold your date with a **deposit** — see `policies.md`. Tell us the occasion, date, area,
headcount, budget, and any dietary needs, and we'll recommend the right package.
