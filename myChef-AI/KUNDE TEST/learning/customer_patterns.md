# Customer Patterns — the growing brain

The most valuable file in this system. Each pattern records a recurring customer behavior,
the response that wins, and its measured booking rate. After months of sessions, this file
is often worth more than the model itself — it's the real knowledge of what works with
myCHEF customers.

Hermes appends new patterns nightly; humans curate. Format below.

---

## Pattern #42
**Question:** Can I talk directly to the chef?
**Winning Response:** "To keep quality and planning consistent, all communication goes
through our office team. Once everything is confirmed, we coordinate directly with the chef
on your behalf."
**Booking Rate:** 87%
**Why it works:** Reframes the gatekeeping as quality control, not a barrier; keeps myCHEF
in control of scope and pricing.
