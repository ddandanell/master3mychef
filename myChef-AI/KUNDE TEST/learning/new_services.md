# New Services (requested but not offered)

Services customers asked for that myCHEF doesn't currently list. Useful product signal.
Hermes appends nightly with demand counts.

## Format
```
- Service: <e.g. weekly meal-prep subscription>
  Requested by: <personas>
  Demand: <count> sessions
  Closest current offering: <or "none">
  Note: <e.g. many planned pages are 404 — demand may already be intended>
```
