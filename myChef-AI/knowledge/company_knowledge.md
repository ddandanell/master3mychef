## Company identity

myCHEF is a premium private chef, villa dining, catering, and hospitality staffing service in Bali.

The company serves people living in Bali or staying in villas, especially:
- Expat families
- Digital nomads
- Villa owners
- Villa guests
- Long-stay households
- Retreats, weddings, events, and premium private parties

myCHEF removes the hassle of finding, checking, coordinating, and replacing chefs or service staff.

## Core positioning

myCHEF is not just a chef directory. It is a managed hospitality solution.

The company helps clients with:
- Private chef bookings
- Villa dining
- Fine dining in villas
- Catering
- Wedding catering
- Retreat catering
- Event chef staffing
- Long-term household chef matching
- Full-time and part-time villa chef staffing
- Waiters, bartenders, butlers, and service staff
- Hospitality staffing for villas and events

## Chef and staff model

Chefs are independent freelance professionals who are vetted before matching.

The vetting can include:
- Identity checks
- Experience checks
- Reference checks
- Skill fit
- Household fit
- Cuisine fit
- Reliability fit

Many chefs may also have hotel, restaurant, villa, or event experience.

## What myCHEF solves

Clients usually do not want to manage the messy part:
- Searching for chefs
- Checking if the chef is real and reliable
- Comparing prices
- Explaining allergies and household routines again and again
- Replacing a chef when someone is unavailable
- Coordinating staff for events
- Managing service staff, timing, setup, and cleanup

myCHEF handles the coordination and keeps continuity.

## Household profile concept

For recurring or longer-stay clients, myCHEF should maintain a household profile.

This can include:
- Food preferences
- Allergies
- Dietary restrictions
- Children and family routines
- Preferred breakfast/lunch/dinner style
- Spice level
- Shopping preferences
- Kitchen setup
- Staff communication preferences
- Previous chef feedback

This makes future bookings smoother and reduces repeated questions.

## Service boundaries

The chef is not a babysitter or cleaner.

The chef can handle food-related duties based on the agreed scope, such as:
- Menu planning
- Grocery planning or shopping if included
- Cooking
- Plating
- Meal prep
- Kitchen cleanup related to cooking

For wider household duties, villa staff or service staff should be scoped separately.
