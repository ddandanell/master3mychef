# myCHEF Staffing Price Knowledge

Last updated: 2026-06-08
Source scope: public myCHEF website search results and available indexed pages. Some requested staffing URLs were not fetchable/indexed during extraction, so missing page-level prices are marked as NOT FOUND instead of invented.

## Global pricing rules for Peter

- Do not give a final quote without confirming the role, dates, location, hours, number of guests or household size, and service complexity.
- For fast qualification, give a ballpark only.
- If the guest asks for catering, villa dining, weddings, or events, use known public starting points below.
- If the guest asks for long-term staffing or recruitment, say pricing depends on schedule, role, experience level, trial, replacement terms, and whether payroll guidance is included.
- Always mention that the booking/date is only locked after a 25% deposit if the client is ready to move forward.

## Known public pricing from myCHEF sources

### Private chef / villa dining guide
Public indexed content says private chef pricing depends on guest count, menu complexity, service style, and whether wine pairing is included.

Known indexed example from Canggu guide:
- Single dinner for 4 to 6 guests: from USD 100 chef fee, plus groceries.
- Groceries: USD 35 to 50 per person.
- Weekly booking: USD 175 per day for daily chef service.

### Weddings and villa events
Public indexed wedding page states:
- Wedding catering and coordination: from IDR 600K per person.
- Includes chef, waiters, coordinator, and one contract.

### In-villa service staff
Public indexed in-villa service page says:
- Service can include waiters, butlers, and mixologists.
- Pricing is based on guest count, timing, tone, role mix, and required staffing level.
- No exact public price found in indexed extract.

## Safe quote language for Peter

Use this when pricing is not public:

"For that setup, I can give you a fast ballpark once I know the dates, hours, villa area, and whether you need chef only or full service staff. Staffing prices move a lot depending on shift length and chef level, so I do not want to throw you a fake number."

Use this when a client asks for a private dinner:

"As a rough starting point, chef-only villa dinners can start around a USD 100 chef fee plus groceries, usually around USD 35 to 50 per person depending on the menu. If you want full service with staff, setup, and premium plating, I will price it properly once I know the guest count and date."

Use this when a client asks for wedding/event catering:

"For wedding-style catering, our public starting point is from IDR 600K per person, then it moves based on menu, staffing, setup, and coordination. Give me the date, guest count, villa area, and style, and I can qualify it fast."
