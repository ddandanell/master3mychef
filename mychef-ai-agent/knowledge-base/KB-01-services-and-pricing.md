## 2. SERVICE-BY-SERVICE REFERENCE

### 2.1 Private Chef Hire (day rate)

**What it is:** You hire the chef team for a stay. **Minimum is a full day of staff** (the existing three-meal rate). You get three meals; assign each as breakfast, lunch or dinner however you want. Groceries are purchased separately at cost with the original receipts. One lunch, dinner or party is **catering** (food included) — not a stay-chef SKU. We inspect the villa and kitchen first so the kitchen meets our standard; we arrive with the team, not a full catering kit.

**Who it suits:** Families and groups staying in a villa; anyone who wants a chef for the stay, not one sitting.

**Team:** 1 professional head chef + 1 kitchen assistant. Every private chef booking includes an assistant.

**Guest coverage:** The published full-day rate covers about `10 guests`. Over 10: extra assistant per +10 and +40% of the base day rate per step.

**Prices:** Stay minimum `IDR 2,700,000++` per day (`IDR 3,267,000` all-in). Weekly −10% / monthly −20% on that full-day rate only. Do not quote `IDR 1,000,000++` / `IDR 1,210,000` as a stay-chef entry.

**Included:** menu planning, food preparation, cooking, serving, and cleaning the kitchen after each meal. Verbatim: *"They will take care of the menu planning, food preparation, cooking, serving, and cleaning the kitchen after each meal."*

**Excluded:** groceries (at cost, receipts provided, no markup); transport where applicable; service charge and VAT on `++` prices.

**Add-ons:** day-before villa visit and menu planning session `IDR 200,000`.

**Cuisine specialists available:** Indonesian, Balinese, Japanese, Chinese, Western, Italian, Mediterranean, Asian fusion, sushi.

### 2.2 Fine Dining

**What it is:** *"Fine Dining is best for a special one-time dinner. We create the menu, bring the ingredients, cook at your villa, serve the courses, and handle everything for you."* Courses are plated and served individually, restaurant-style.

**Who it suits:** Special one-off occasions — anniversaries, proposals, milestone birthdays, VIP dinners.

**Team:** 1 master/head chef + 1–2 assistants.

**Guest minimum:** **6 guests.** `our minimum booking is six guests`.

**Prices:** `IDR 950,000++` to `IDR 2,200,000++` per guest depending on the menu (see table 1.2). Children aged 4 to 11 at 50%.

**Included:** all fresh ingredients, preparation, individual course service, kitchen organisation, complete cleanup, and a villa inspection coordinated with villa management before the event.

**Excluded:** groceries are **included** in per-guest fine-dining pricing (unlike chef hire). Alcohol is never included. Transport to remote areas is charged separately. Service charge and VAT on top of `++` prices. Note the recorded under-delivery incident: **the table itself is not included** in outdoor setups.

**Villa inspection is mandatory** for fine dining.

## 3. PRICING RULES

### 3.1 Groceries — cost-at-cost with receipts, no markup

Groceries are **always excluded** on chef-hire (day-rate) bookings and are charged at the exact purchase price, with all original receipts handed over. There is no markup.

Verbatim lines in rotation:
- `Groceries are charged separately at the actual purchase price, and you receive all receipts.`
- `you only pay what it cost from the supermarket you will receive the receipts.`
- `we don't add any markup to the groceries`
- `We keep the grocery cost completely transparent — there is no markup on the groceries. You will simply pay the actual cost of the ingredients, and the chef will provide the grocery receipt afterward.`
- `Groceries are charged separately at cost, depending on the menu you choose, so you only pay for the ingredients you would like us to buy.`

**Grocery deposit:** `For the groceries, we would usually request a deposit of around IDR 500,000–1,000,000, depending on the menu and ingredients you choose.` Any remaining balance is `returned/reconciled accordingly`. One booking used `Deposit groceries IDR 800.000`; one booking had the deposit waived.

**Grocery estimate used in small-booking build-ups:** `Estimated groceries: IDR 500,000 to IDR 800,000` (for one meal, two guests).

**Note:** per-guest fine-dining, family-style and BBQ pricing **includes** ingredients. Groceries-at-cost applies to chef hire.

### 3.2 The 6-guest minimum

- `our minimum booking is six guests`
- `For dinner-only bookings, we normally have a minimum of six guests.`
- `our BBQ setup has a minimum charge of 6 guests`
- `our breakfast service has a minimum of six guests`
- `For events, the minimum booking is six guests. Every private chef booking includes an assistant.`

**Rationale line, verbatim:** *"Sending a head chef and assistant involves almost the same preparation, transport, and working time regardless of whether they cook for two or six guests."*

**Exception on record:** a dedicated standalone BBQ grill setup carries a **10-guest** minimum — `It's minimum 10 people` + `It's a big job setting up`.

### 3.3 The BBQ all-in floor (`IDR 6,050,000`)

The BBQ minimum-charge principle is stated verbatim as: *"our BBQ setup has a minimum charge of 6 guests. For 2 people, the setup cost is almost the same because we still need to send the chef, assistant/service staff, grill setup, transport, preparation, cooking, service, and cleanup. … This would be the minimum booking price for the seafood BBQ, even if it is only for 2 guests."*

The base figure recorded in the corpus is: `Seafood BBQ minimum package: 6 guests × IDR 750,000 = IDR 4,500,000`.

**⚠️ The `IDR 6,050,000` all-in BBQ floor is NOT stated verbatim anywhere in the research.** `IDR 4,500,000 × 1.21` (10% service + 10% tax) does compute to `IDR 5,445,000`, not `IDR 6,050,000`; `IDR 5,000,000 × 1.21` computes to `IDR 6,050,000`. **The assistant must not quote an "IDR 6,050,000 all-in BBQ floor" until an owner confirms it.** See Section 4, conflict 8.

### 3.4 Service charge, VAT, and the `++` notation

`++` means service charge and tax are added on top of the stated price.

- Explainer used to customers: `Yes, there is a service charge and VAT on top of the price, but other than that, everything is included.`
- One definition given: `Yes, the "++" refers to a 10% service charge and 10% VAT.`
- Another definition given: `Total including 10% service and 11% VAT` / `plus 10% service and 11% tax`.

**What the arithmetic actually does:** every computed total in the corpus uses **×1.21 (10% + 10%)**:
- `1,000,000 → 1,210,000`
- `1,800,000 → 2,178,000`
- `2,700,000 → 3,267,000`
- `29,212,500 → 35,347,125`

Only the 9 Jul seafood-BBQ quote and the Doug fine-dining proposal claim 11% VAT. The Doug proposal states `The totals include 10% service charge and 11% VAT.`

**Additional defect flagged in the research:** the `++` explainer contains an **arithmetic error** — 10% VAT is applied to the base price rather than to the service-charged subtotal. Correct compounding would be `base × 1.10 × 1.10 = ×1.21` (which happens to match), but the explainer's step-by-step shows VAT computed off the base (e.g. cooking class: `10% service charge: IDR 280,000 → 10% VAT: IDR 308,000`, where `308,000` is 11% of the base rather than 10% of `3,080,000`).

### 3.5 Deposit, payment and lead time

- `A 50% deposit confirms the booking.`
- `The remaining 50% is due on the day of the event.`
- Last-minute bookings: **full prepayment** — `As this is a last-minute booking, we require the full payment to confirm the reservation.`
- Payment rails: bank transfer preferred, cash accepted, Revolut offered. `At the moment, we do not offer card payments, as card processing for catering companies in Indonesia can be quite complicated.` No PayPal.
- Booking lead time is stated **inconsistently** as 1–2 days, 2–3 days, or at least 1 week. See Section 4.

### 3.6 Villa inspection

Always performed pre-service, coordinated directly with villa management. Framed as **included in the service**, not as a requirement. Requires the villa management contact number. Where a guest is on Airbnb with no villa-manager contact, **kitchen photos** are accepted instead.
