# KB-02 — myCHEF Bali: Qualification and Booking Flow

**Source:** myCHEF Bali WhatsApp corpus analysis, 2 Jul – 12 Aug 2026.
This file governs **what to ask, in what order, and when it is safe to quote.**

---

## 1. THE HARD RULE

> **Never quote a firm price before you know: guest count + location/area + date + kitchen status (and service type).**

The research states it plainly: *"Rule for any assistant: never quote without at least service type + guests + date + area."* Staff almost never quote without the core five qualifiers.

**What IS allowed earlier:** a **ballpark range**, clearly labelled as indicative. The corpus contains exactly this pattern for a 20-guest enquiry: `an investment starting from approximately IDR 750,000 to 1,500,000+ per person`. Ranges of this kind may be given before full qualification, provided they are framed as an estimate and immediately followed by the qualifying questions.

**What is NOT allowed:** a firm per-guest price, a firm total, an all-in figure, or a discount, before the four facts are known. Transport fees depend on area, minimums depend on guest count, availability depends on date, and the format recommendation depends on kitchen status — quoting early has repeatedly produced quotes that had to be walked back.

## 2. THE QUALIFICATION LADDER — exact questions, in the order staff ask them

### Core five (asked before any firm quote)

**1. Service type**
> `Are you looking for a fine-dining experience, a family-style dinner, or to rent a private chef with a service team?`

Variants in use:
> `How can I help you today? Are you looking for a private chef, fine dining experience, family-style dinner, or BBQ?`
> `What type of food or experience are you looking for?`
> `What type of dining experience are you interested in? (e.g., fine dining, family-style dinner, private chef hire, BBQ/catering)`

This is asked in virtually every first reply.

**2. Date(s)**
> `What date are you considering?`
> `When are you looking forward?`
> `Could you also please let me know your preferred date and the location of your villa?`
> `Before I can confirm the booking, I just need to know the exact dates, as we already have several bookings and I need to check which chef team is available.`
> `For which date are you planning your event?`

**3. Number of guests** (split adults vs children — ages matter, kids 4–11 are charged at 50%)
> `How many guests will there be?`
> `How many people are you?`
> `How many guests will be attending?`

**4. Area / villa name** (drives the transport fee **and** the villa inspection)
> `Which area or villa will you be staying in?`
> `Where are you located?`
> `Please also send me the villa name or location, and I will prepare the best option for you.`
> `Which area is your villa located in?`
> `Which villa or area in Bali will this take place?`
> Bahasa: `Untuk lokasi dimana kak?`

**5. Cuisine preference + allergies / dietary requirements**
> `What type of food or experience are you looking for?`
> `Could you please let me know what kind of food you're looking for, so I can create a menu that fits what you need?`
> `Do you have any allergies I need to know so I can plan the menu around that.`
> `Would you prefer Italian, Indonesian, Japanese, or another cuisine? This will help me recommend the most suitable menu and chef for you.`
> `Do you have any cuisine preferences or dietary requirements?`

### Secondary questions, asked when relevant

**6. Meals per day** — drives the chef-hire tier (1 / 2 / 3 meals).
> `For private chef we have a few different options depending on how many meals you would like each day`

**7. Kitchen adequacy** — part of the hard rule; must be established before a firm quote.
> `Do you have a proper kitchen to do the cooking?`
> `Is there a kitchen at the villa, or will we need to set one up?`

For groups of ~45 the kitchen question is pre-empted: *"We usually bring and set up our own cooking station, so we don't need a fully equipped kitchen."*

**8. Add-ons** — bartender, cocktails, wine, decorations, cake.
> `Are you also looking for a bartender and cocktail service for the event, or would you only require the food and BBQ service?`
> `Will you handle the drinks yourselves, or would you like us to set up a bar with bartenders as well?`
> `Do you need us to provide plates, cutlery and glasses?`
> `Would you like our team to serve the food, or is a buffet setup enough?`

**9. Villa management contact number** — required before service; triggers the inspection.
> `Hi, could you please share the Villa manager number ? Just wanna ask them if we could do that event there.`
> `And also we need your name, villa name also villa's management contact to check the equipment there`

**10. Occasion** — birthday / anniversary / proposal. Used to upsell romantic setups, cakes, decorations.

**11. Agency or direct client?**
> `May I also ask if you are contacting us as an agency or are you booking directly as the client?`
> followed by → `What is your company name, and have you already used us before?`

**12. Kids' menu?**
> `For the children, would you like a separate kids' menu or the same menu as the adults?`

## 3. HEADCOUNT → SERVICE FORMAT MAPPING

| Guest count | Recommended format | Reasoning / verbatim support |
|---|---|---|
| **2 guests** | **Full-day private chef hire** (`IDR 2,700,000++` / `IDR 3,267,000` all-in) | Below the 6-guest dinner minimum. Steer honestly: *"We could charge the six-guest minimum, but I honestly don't think that would make financial sense for you. You would receive much better value from the full-day package."* Do **not** lead with the minimum. |
| **2–4 guests** | Private chef hire (day rate), or cooking class (flat rate covers up to 4) | Fine dining and dinner-only family style have a 6-guest minimum. For a single special dinner below 6, offer full-day chef hire, or the waitlist template if the date is peak. Romantic 7-course for two exists as a fixed package (`IDR 6,950,000 for two guests`). |
| **4–5 guests** | Private chef hire; or dinner-only only if the customer accepts the 6-guest minimum charge | Same logic as above. |
| **6+ guests** | Fine dining, family style, or BBQ — all unlocked at 6 | `our minimum booking is six guests`. BBQ charge minimum is also 6. |
| **10+ guests** | Live BBQ with dedicated grill setup becomes viable | `It's minimum 10 people` for a standalone dedicated BBQ setup — `It's a big job setting up`. |
| **~18–20 guests** | Buffet, BBQ, or mixed BBQ + buffet | `Untuk 20 pax bisa di buffet`. Mixed BBQ + buffet for 18 quoted at `IDR 850,000++ per guest`. 20-pax ballpark: `an investment starting from approximately IDR 750,000 to 1,500,000+ per person`. |
| **~40–50 guests** | **BBQ or buffet with own cooking station**, plus group discount | *"For groups of around 45 guests, we normally recommend a BBQ or buffet setup, as it works best for larger events. We usually bring and set up our own cooking station, so we don't need a fully equipped kitchen."* Team scales to 1 head chef + 3–4 assistants. Group discounts: 20% max (43–45 pax), 10% tier, 5% (33 pax). |
| **Recurring / multi-week / full-time** | Long-stay retainer with rotating specialist chef teams | `Monthly Investment: IDR 32,000,000` — two chefs, rotating 7-day schedule, fully managed. Cuisine rotated week by week. 10% long-stay discount observed on an 11-day booking. |
| **Multi-day stay (any size)** | Private chef hire day rate, not per-dinner pricing | *"This normally becomes the more economical option if you want breakfast, lunch and/or dinner rather than just one meal."* |

**Format explainer to send when the customer doesn't know which to choose** (verbatim):
> Fine Dining is best for a special one-time dinner. We create the menu, bring the ingredients, cook at your villa, serve the courses, and handle everything for you.
> Family Style is more relaxed. The chef prepares several dishes that are placed on the table for everyone to share rather than serving individual courses.
> Private Chef Hire is different. You hire the chef for a longer period, usually for several meals or most of the day.

## 4. THE FULL BOOKING FLOW — first message to confirmed booking

### Stage 0 — Inbound arrives
An auto-acknowledgement fires on any inbound (see KB-03, T1/T2). **Known bug:** the auto-ack has fired as the reply to a substantive pricing question, and has fired a second time after a human had already replied. A human/AI reply must follow, not replace, the auto-ack.

### Stage 1 — First touch (master qualifier)
Send the greeting + service-type question + "once I have this, I'll send options and prices". Add date, guest count and location asks where the enquiry is fully open. (KB-03 T3 / T3b / T3c / T4 / T5.)

### Stage 2 — Qualify
Collect the core five: service type, date, guest count, area/villa, cuisine + dietary. Add kitchen status, meals per day, and add-ons as relevant. A ballpark range may be offered here; a firm quote may not.

### Stage 3 — Check availability
Either confirm availability directly, or send a holding message and come back:
> `Give me 20 minutes I will get back to you. I'm just checking with a different head chef to see what is possible.`
> `Could you please give me a moment? I just need to recheck and confirm with the chef. I'll get back to you as soon as I hear from the chef. Thank you for your patience!`

### Stage 4 — Quote / proposal
Name the option before pricing it. Present 2–3 options. Itemise. End on exactly one question. Long proposals use ALL-CAPS section headers (`PERSONALISED PROPOSAL`, `WHAT IS INCLUDED`, `OPTION 1: …`, `PRIVATE COCKTAIL SERVICE`, `Complete investment`). Attach the villa-inspection block. Optionally attach the menu link with the "don't look at the prices on that page" caveat (KB-03 T19).

Soft-urgency close, verbatim:
> `Bookings are coming in quickly, so I would appreciate your confirmation as soon as possible. I can then prepare the quotation and deposit invoice to secure your preferred date.`

### Stage 5 — Deposit and invoice
> `Once you confirm the booking, we will send you an invoice for a 50% deposit. After the deposit is received, we will arrange a site inspection to check the location, discuss where everything should be set up, and make sure the event runs smoothly.`
> `The remaining 50% is due on the day of the event.`

- Payment: bank transfer preferred; cash accepted; Revolut offered; **no card payments**; no PayPal.
- **Last-minute bookings require 100% prepayment** before confirmation.
- Bank details block is in KB-03 (T14e). **Risk flag: the account is in a personal name while high-value customers ask for company name + NPWP. Escalate all such requests to a human.**

### Stage 6 — Details collection to lock the booking
Before finalising, collect (verbatim from the live checklist):
> • Villa name & location
> • Villa management contact number — we'll contact them to confirm that a private chef is allowed at the villa.
> • If you're already at the villa, could you please send us a few photos of the kitchen, including the cooking area and available equipment such as pans, pots, knives, etc.? This will help us make sure the chef has everything needed.

> `Once we have these details, we can proceed with the booking and send you the payment details for the grocery deposit.`

### Stage 7 — Villa inspection
Arranged after the deposit, coordinated directly with villa management. Checks kitchen, gas, oven, pans, plates, glassware, electricity, water, access, serving area, and overall setup. Framed as **included in the service**, never as a hurdle. Where no villa manager exists (e.g. Airbnb), **kitchen photos** are accepted instead.

### Stage 8 — Menu finalisation and grocery deposit
Menu agreed with the customer; grocery deposit of around `IDR 500,000–1,000,000` requested; groceries purchased at cost with receipts, balance reconciled.

### Stage 9 — Payment confirmation
> `Thank you. We confirm that we have received your payment, and your cooking class booking is now confirmed.`

### Stage 10 — Service day, then post-service
Post-service thank-you + review request (KB-03 T23), review link `https://g.page/r/CcIKSelkhzGEEAE/review`.

### Stage 11 — Follow-up if the lead goes quiet
Follow-up nudge after roughly 48 hours (KB-03 T6).

## 5. WHAT MUST BE COLLECTED BEFORE HANDING OFF TO A HUMAN

The handoff packet must contain **all** of the following:

**Mandatory before any firm quote or handoff:**
1. Customer name
2. Service type requested (fine dining / family style / private chef hire / BBQ / buffet / cooking class / bar / other)
3. Date(s) — exact, including whether it is a peak date (Christmas, New Year)
4. Guest count — split into adults and children, with children's ages (4–11 bracket matters)
5. Area / villa name
6. Kitchen status — proper kitchen available, or cooking station needed
7. Cuisine preference
8. Allergies and dietary requirements

**Collect as soon as available:**
9. Meals per day (for chef hire)
10. Villa management contact number
11. Occasion (birthday / anniversary / proposal)
12. Add-ons wanted: bartender, cocktails, cake, decorations, kids' menu
13. Whether the enquirer is an **agency** or a **direct client**, and their company name
14. Budget, if the customer volunteers one
15. The `(Ref: MC-XXXXXX)` code if present in the thread

**Escalate to a human immediately — do not attempt to resolve:**
- Any request for an itemised invoice, company name, NPWP, or a business bank account
- Any pricing question that touches an item flagged in KB-01 Section 4 (unresolved conflicts) — especially website-price-vs-quote discrepancies and the Surf & Turf 950k/1,000k/1,350k spread
- Standalone butler enquiries (no standalone rate exists)
- Kosher / halal-certified chef requests
- Adult-party environments (topless waitresses, adult novelties) — **policy gap, no ruling exists**
- Enterprise due diligence: CVs, SOPs, NDAs, references, backup staffing guarantees
- Any request outside Bali
- Any complaint, cancellation, refund or under-delivery issue
