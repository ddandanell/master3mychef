# KB-04 — myCHEF Bali: Objections and Edge Cases

**Source:** myCHEF Bali WhatsApp corpus, 2 Jul – 12 Aug 2026.

**How this file is organised:**
- **Part A — Objections that have a proven verbatim response.** Customer words verbatim, staff reply verbatim.
- **Part B — Edge cases.** Where a working response exists it is quoted; where none exists the section is marked **NO TEMPLATE EXISTS — DRAFT PROPOSED** and a draft in the house voice follows.
- **Part C — Escalate-to-human list.**

**Core principle observed throughout the corpus:** *answer every price objection with a cost breakdown, not a discount.*

---

# PART A — OBJECTIONS WITH A PROVEN RESPONSE

## A1 — Budget gap, large group (customer budget IDR 15,000,000 vs quote IDR 25,000,000+)

**Customer position:** budget is around IDR 15,000,000 for a 43–45 guest BBQ.

**Staff response — hold the price, itemise the value (verbatim core):**
```
I completely understand your budget is around IDR 15,000,000. However, we would not be able to provide our required quality and service standard within that budget.

The price covers much more than the food itself. We use fresh, high-quality meat, seafood, and ingredients, which represent a significant part of the cost. For an event of this size, we also provide:

• One professional chef and four assistants
• Villa inspection and event planning
• Ingredient sourcing and preparation
• Professional grill transportation and setup
• Live cooking at the villa
• Service coordination, pack-up, and complete cleanup

We will inspect the villa beforehand to check the kitchen, grill area, electricity, water, access, and service flow. This allows us to plan everything properly and provide a smooth, high-quality experience for all your guests.

The 20% group discount is already the best price we can offer without reducing the quality of the ingredients or the service team.

If the budget is flexible, I can send you the complete menus and prepare a formal quotation.
```
(Full template = KB-03 T13b. **This is the canonical value-stack rebuttal.**)

## A5 — Below-minimum guest count (general)

**Best response:** KB-03 **T17 — the "honest" below-minimum steer.** Key line (verbatim):
```
We could charge the six-guest minimum, but I honestly don't think that would make financial sense for you. You would receive much better value from the full-day package.
```

Supporting rationale (verbatim):
```
For dinner-only bookings, we normally have a minimum of six guests. Sending a head chef and assistant involves almost the same preparation, transport, and working time regardless of whether they cook for two or six guests.
```

**Do NOT lead with the minimum.** KB-03 T7c does exactly that and is flagged as the worst opener in the corpus.

## A7 — Below-minimum **and** peak date

**Best response:** KB-03 **T15g — the waitlist template.** Verbatim:
```
Thank you for reaching out. We would love to help, but 24 December is one of our busiest Christmas dates, and our minimum booking is six guests. Unfortunately, we're unable to confirm this booking for four guests at the moment.

If it is okay with you, I can place your request on our waiting list. If we receive a cancellation or another opportunity becomes available within the next week, I will contact you immediately. Availability is limited, so I cannot guarantee it, but I will do my best to help.

Alternatively, if you are flexible with the date, we would be happy to arrange the dinner on another day.
```

**This waitlist + alternative-date pattern is the strongest decline structure in the corpus and should be the default.**

## A14 — Too-late notice

**Templates:** KB-03 T15a–T15i. **Best pattern = waitlist + alternative date (T15g).**

**Counter-intuitive data point:** the bluntest version (`Sorry guys, not possible…`, T15a) offered no alternative — yet that same lead **returned the next day and converted.** Keep the polite versions as default, but a firm decline does not necessarily lose the lead.

**⚠️ Lead-time policy is stated three different ways** — 1–2 days, 2–3 days, at least 1 week. See KB-01 Section 4, conflict 9. Until this is ruled on, prefer `at least 2 to 3 days in advance`, which is the most common.

# PART B — EDGE CASES

## B1 — Below-minimum guest count
**Covered.** Use KB-03 T17 (honest steer to full-day chef hire) or T15g (waitlist) if the date is peak. See A5–A7.

## B2 — Budget far below the floor
**Covered.** Large group → A1 value-stack. Small group / hopeless budget → A2 polite walk-away. Mid-gap → A3 format downsell.

## B3 — Same-day / next-day requests
**Covered.** KB-03 T15a–T15e, T15i. Same-day is sometimes accepted with a surcharge: `Same-day short notice surcharge = IDR 500,000` (4 guests, Ubud, total `IDR 3,300,000`). Last-minute bookings require **100% prepayment** (KB-03 T14b). Enthusiastic same-day acceptances on record: `Haiii yes it's possible for tonight`.

## B4 — Out-of-area requests
**Covered for Jakarta / outside Bali:** KB-03 T15f. Verbatim: `We do open our services in Jakarta during selected periods throughout the year, but right now our full focus and team capacity are here in Bali.`

**Remote areas within Bali** (Medewi, Ubud, Uluwatu) are served with transport surcharges — see KB-01 table 1.6. Note the Uluwatu figure is **conflicting** (300k / 400k / 500k).

## B5 — Medical / accessibility dietary needs

**What exists:** allergy and dietary questions are asked routinely — `Do you have any allergies I need to know so I can plan the menu around that.` — and dietary requirements are honoured in planning (`plan everything precisely around your preferences and dietary requirements`; `while taking his dietary requirements into account`). Vegetarian is handled without surcharge: `There is no additional charge for the vegetarian menu.` A 2-day booking for `4 vegetarian adults` was quoted normally.

**What does NOT exist:** kosher and halal-certified. Verbatim customer message, **never answered**:
```
Hi, I'd like to know if you have a recommendation for a Casher Private Chef ?
```
A halal alcohol-free menu request was also left unanswered.

### NO TEMPLATE EXISTS — DRAFT PROPOSED (medical / strict-observance dietary needs)

```
Hi [Name], thank you for contacting myCHEF Bali.

My name is Dede, Event Coordinator at myCHEF Bali.

Thank you for telling me about this up front, it genuinely helps.

We plan every menu around allergies, intolerances and dietary requirements as standard, and there is no additional charge for a vegetarian menu. Our chefs prepare the menu with you before the service, so we can build the whole day around what you can and cannot eat.

For strict religious certification, such as kosher or certified halal, I want to be honest with you rather than promise something we cannot guarantee. Let me confirm with our head chef team what we can properly deliver, and I will come back to you today with a clear answer.

Could you please let me know:

• The exact requirement, and whether certification is needed or careful preparation is enough
• Any allergies, including severity
• Your date, number of guests, and the area your villa is in

Best regards,
Dede
Event Coordinator | myCHEF Bali
```
**Then escalate to a human. Never confirm kosher or certified-halal capability without an owner ruling.**
