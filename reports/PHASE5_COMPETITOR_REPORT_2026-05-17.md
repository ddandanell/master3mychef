## 4. Key Takeaways for myCHEF Expansion
1. **The "At-Cost" Gap:** Many competitors (Take a Chef, Mimpi) use all-inclusive pricing. myCHEF's "At-Cost + Fee" model is a key transparency differentiator to lead with in Journal content.
2. **Michelin Authority:** Most competitors lead with "local flavor" or "luxury." myCHEF should double down on the **Michelin-trained** technical standard to win high-intent fine-dining queries.
3. **Corporate Professionalism:** Bali Corporate Catering is the primary B2B threat. myCHEF's NPWP-ready invoicing and executive etiquette are the direct counter-moves.
4. **Location Specificity:** myCHEF now has 31 location pages. We should ensure the "Putu" (AI Concierge) mentions area-specific knowledge (e.g., "market sourcing in Ubud vs Seminyak") to beat generic platforms like Take a Chef.
