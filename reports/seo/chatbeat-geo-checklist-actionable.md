## 17 Key Takeaways (Chatbeat doctrine)

Use these as the strategy filter for all GEO work:

1. Google is bleeding clicks → AI Overviews + ChatGPT steal SERP traffic  
2. Brand ranking is influenceable via mentions + technical upgrades  
3. **Open site for AI crawlers** (OpenAI, Perplexity, etc.)  
4. Enable AI training on **YouTube transcripts**  
5. Know **sources that shape AI answers** and show up there  
6. **Mentions = fuel** (quality > volume)  
7. **Reddit** is a primary LLM training/citation playground  
8. Structure content (schema, summaries) or stay invisible  
9. Traditional SEO still is the bedrock of GEO  
10. Control narrative on reviews, bios, directories (Wikipedia/Crunchbase/etc.)  
11. ChatGPT reads YouTube  
12. Optional: own Custom GPT as a surface  
13. Slow pages hurt  
14. Real mentions > classic backlinks alone  
15. Use ChatGPT Agent / Deep Research to force crawl of your content  
16. Don’t spam communities — be helpful  
17. Social listening (Brand24 etc.) to join real conversations
