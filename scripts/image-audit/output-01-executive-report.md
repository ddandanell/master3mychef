## 2. What myCHEF.id Does

The company is a mobile hospitality operator, not a marketplace or app. It delivers restaurant-level food and service inside private Bali villas. The offer is organised into four pillars:

1. **Private Fine Dining** — 24 set menus (vegetarian, seafood, mixed meats, single-meat), multi-course tasting experiences, Chef’s Table and romantic dinners. Minimums typically from 6 guests; pricing from IDR 1.25M per guest.
2. **Catering** — BBQ, buffet, plated set menus, drop-off, Babi Guling, grazing tables, floating breakfast, villa catering, and corporate/retreat catering.
3. **Events** — Villa weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events, wellness/yoga retreats, baby showers and villa parties, run as full "productions" with chefs, waiters, bartenders and a coordinator.
4. **Private Experiences** — Six bookable experiences: Private Cocktail Party, Sushi Masterclass, Private Cooking Class, Kids Birthday Chef Party, Champagne & Oyster Experience, and Romantic Proposal Dinner.

Secondary services include in-villa staffing, long-term chef and villa staff placement, VIP transport, B2B bar services for venues, and bundled all-inclusive packages: the **Complete Villa Experience** and **Villa Event Packages**.

## 10. Implications for Image Generation

Replacement photography must reinforce the four service pillars while staying inside verifiable facts. Show Balinese villa settings (pool, terrace, jungle pavilion, clifftop, garden bale), golden-hour and candlelit lighting, professional staff in clean neutral/white attire, and food that reads as Italian-fine-dining-meets-Balinese-sourcing. People should match the occasion: couples, families, children, corporate groups. Favour invisible service moments — a waiter pouring wine, a chef plating at a villa counter, a bartender shaking a cocktail, a table being reset — over staged poses.

Avoid: text/logos in photographs, vehicles with unknown branding, named awards, invented uniforms, non-Bali architecture, or imagery that implies Adriano personally cooks every meal.
