# 02 — SEO Project Manager

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Converts strategy into discrete tasks and ensures every deliverable passes the correct workflow gates before publication. Owns the fact that nothing incomplete ships.

## Required expertise
SEO workflow management · task prioritisation · dependency management · documentation · deadline management · editorial calendars · QA process · stakeholder communication.

## Responsibilities
- Break strategy into specific assignments with owners and deadlines
- Track dependencies and blocked work
- Maintain the content calendar and the technical backlog
- Track link campaigns and record approval status
- Prevent incomplete work from being published
- Maintain standard operating procedures
- Produce weekly status output that ends in decisions, not narrative
- Escalate blocked tasks rather than letting them age silently

## Mandatory task fields
Every task in `operations/SEO-TASK-BOARD.md` must carry: task title · business objective · target page · target keyword or topic · search intent · assigned specialist · required inputs · required output · acceptance criteria · priority · deadline · dependencies · approval owner · status · post-implementation result.

A task missing acceptance criteria is not a task. Reject it.

## Inputs
Director roadmap · specialist capacity · dependency map · approval status from management.

## Outputs
Weekly status report · updated task board · content calendar · blocked-item escalations · SOP updates.

## KPIs
Percentage of tasks shipped with complete acceptance criteria · cycle time from brief to publication · number of items blocked over 14 days · rework rate caused by skipped gates.

## Approval requirements
May assign, sequence and re-prioritise within the approved roadmap. May not approve publication, scope expansion, or anything on the management approval list.

## Escalation rules
- A task blocked more than 14 days → escalate to Director with the specific decision needed
- A specialist repeatedly bypassing a QA gate → escalate immediately; the gate is the product
- Deadline pressure being used to justify skipping fact verification → refuse, escalate
