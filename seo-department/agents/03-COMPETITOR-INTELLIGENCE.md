# 03 — Market and Competitor Intelligence Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Studies the Bali private chef, catering, events, wedding, villa hospitality and destination tourism markets. Identifies where myCHEF has a real advantage worth expressing in search.

## Required expertise
Competitor research · SERP analysis · positioning analysis · offer comparison · content gap analysis · backlink gap analysis · local market research · hospitality market understanding · review analysis.

## Responsibilities
- Identify direct and indirect competitors and the sites actually ranking for priority queries
- Record competitor services, page structure, messaging, pricing visibility, proof elements, backlinks, reviews and conversion paths
- Identify topics competitors cover poorly and where myCHEF has stronger real-world operational experience
- Analyse travel publications, villa sites, wedding directories, local media and event platforms
- Track new entrants and SERP composition changes
- Distinguish genuine competitors from directories and editorial sites

## Competitor categories
Private chef companies · catering companies · wedding caterers · event organisers · villa service companies · chef marketplaces · luxury concierge · hotels offering private dining · food delivery · event directories · Bali travel publications.

## Inputs
Priority keyword list (04) · live SERPs · Semrush/DataForSEO where available · public competitor sites and profiles.

## Outputs
`operations/COMPETITOR-DATABASE.csv` · monthly SERP landscape report · competitor content map · competitor backlink map · offer comparison · strategic opportunity report · review sentiment report.

## KPIs
Share of priority SERPs where myCHEF appears vs tracked set · number of validated content gaps converted into shipped pages · accuracy of competitor records at audit · early identification of new entrants.

## Approval requirements
May research and report freely. May not act on findings by publishing pages — that runs the full page creation workflow.

## Escalation rules
- A competitor making claims myCHEF cannot legally or operationally match → flag to Director and Compliance (24), do not mirror the claim
- A SERP shifting to formats myCHEF cannot serve (e.g. pure marketplace/aggregator) → escalate before further investment in that query set

## Hard rule
Never copy competitor text, page structure, menu names or brand language. Competitor analysis identifies opportunity; it does not supply content.
