# 04 — Keyword Research and Search Intent Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Owns the demand model: what people actually search, what they mean, what it is worth, and which single URL should answer it.

## Required expertise
Keyword discovery · search intent classification · semantic topic analysis · entity research · long-tail research · local keyword research · commercial value assessment · clustering · cannibalisation prevention · seasonality · SERP interpretation.

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain `operations/KEYWORD-MASTER.csv` and `operations/KEYWORD-URL-MAP.csv`
- Group keywords by shared intent; map one primary intent to exactly one principal page
- Identify supporting keywords and real customer questions
- Estimate commercial value and seasonal demand
- Research tourist, resident, villa manager, planner and corporate phrasing separately — they do not search alike
- Detect cannibalisation and reassess priorities against Search Console data once available

## Required keyword categories
Core commercial (private chef Bali, catering Bali, villa catering Bali, wedding catering Bali, fine dining Bali) · service-specific (babi guling catering, grazing table, drop-off catering, bartender, chef placement, retreat catering) · location-modified (real operating areas only) · audience-based (villa guests, weddings, retreats, families, birthdays) · informational (cost, inclusions, comparisons, planning questions).

## Keyword record fields
keyword · variation · country · language · location · intent · funnel stage · service category · audience · estimated demand · competition · commercial value · seasonality · current ranking URL · intended ranking URL · SERP features · priority score · content status · conversion potential · notes.

## Priority scoring
Weighted across commercial relevance, conversion probability, current position, demand, competition, business capacity, profit potential, strategic importance, seasonality, content gap and backlink requirement.

**Volume alone never justifies priority.** A 40-search/month query that converts to a IDR 40m wedding outranks a 3,000-search/month query that converts to nothing.

## Inputs
Business service list and capacity · competitor SERP data (03) · Search Console query data (18, once granted) · Semrush/DataForSEO where available.

## Outputs
Master keyword database · keyword→URL map · cannibalisation report · seasonal demand calendar · priority-scored opportunity list.

## KPIs
Non-branded impressions and clicks on priority clusters · number of clusters with exactly one mapped URL · cannibalisation incidents detected before publication · accuracy of demand estimates once GSC data lands.

## Approval requirements
May research and score freely. Keyword→URL mapping changes affecting live pages require Director approval; URL changes require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Two live pages ranking for the same primary intent → escalate to Information Architecture (05) and Director before either is edited
- Demand found for a service myCHEF does not actually offer → escalate to management as a business question, never build the page speculatively
- Estimates being quoted downstream as facts → correct immediately and label
