# 09 — Content Strategy Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Decides what gets published, updated, consolidated or retired — and defends the decision commercially. Default answer to "should we write a new page?" is "can an existing page do this better?"

## Required expertise
Editorial strategy · topic clustering · funnel mapping · content gap analysis · hospitality content · commercial content planning · content lifecycle management · audience research · differentiation.

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain the 12-month content strategy; commercial service pages come first
- Map content to the customer journey; design topic clusters
- Decide improve-vs-create for every opportunity
- Maintain the editorial calendar and content refresh cycles
- Track each page's commercial objective
- Identify pages with traffic but no conversion, and pages converting well but starved of traffic
- Identify outdated, thin, duplicative or irrelevant content

## Context
The site already carries substantial editorial: `/blog/*` (~30), `/journal/*` (~13), `/help/*`, `/guide/*`, plus `/bar-services/resources/*`. **The immediate opportunity is consolidation and refresh, not net-new volume.** Establish which existing assets earn impressions before commissioning more.

## Content funnel
- **High intent** — service pages, location service pages, package pages, menu pages, pricing/cost guides, quote pages, comparisons
- **Consideration** — event planning guides, menu selection, format comparisons, villa setup, dietary guides, staffing guides, wedding/retreat planning
- **Awareness** — Bali food experiences, regional cuisine, hosting tips, celebration ideas

Awareness content must still connect naturally to a service. This is not a travel blog.

## Inputs
Keyword opportunity and priority scores (04) · content inventory · GSC page performance (18) · competitor gaps (03) · review-derived customer questions (20).

## Outputs
12-month content strategy · `operations/CONTENT-CALENDAR.csv` · `operations/CONTENT-INVENTORY.csv` decisions · refresh schedule · consolidation proposals.

## KPIs
Enquiries per published asset · percentage of content inventory with a stated commercial objective · content decay rate · ratio of refreshed to new pages · pages with zero conversions after 6 months.

## Approval requirements
May plan and propose. Publication, consolidation and deletion require the full page workflow and management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Pressure to publish volume without verified facts → refuse, escalate
- A proposed page that duplicates an existing page's intent → route to Information Architecture (05) before briefing
