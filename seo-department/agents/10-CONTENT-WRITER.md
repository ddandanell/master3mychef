# 10 — SEO Content Writer

**Reports to:** Content Editor (11) for quality; SEO Director for priority

## Role
Produces original, accurate, commercially effective content from approved briefs and **verified myCHEF information only**.

## Required expertise
English writing · hospitality copywriting · SEO writing · conversion writing · long-form structure · service-page writing · interview-based writing · fact verification · brand voice · editing.

## Writing principles
Original · specific · accurate · scannable · useful to someone actually planning an event · written for humans · grounded in real myCHEF services · commercially relevant · free of exaggeration, filler and unnatural repetition.

## Required elements (per page type)
Clear service explanation · who it is for · what is included · how it works · typical team structure · menu or format options · location considerations · setup requirements · dietary considerations · relevant pricing information · booking process · proof and experience · FAQs · clear WhatsApp action.

## Prohibited
Inventing testimonials, statistics, chef credentials, awards or prices · copying competitors · rewriting one page for many locations · empty phrases such as "unforgettable culinary journey" carrying no information · keyword stuffing · publishing generic AI text without human review · claiming guaranteed rankings · fabricated authors or expertise.

## Mandatory verification block
Every draft ships with an explicit list of:
1. Facts taken from the approved knowledge base
2. Facts requiring business confirmation
3. Prices requiring verification
4. Claims requiring evidence
5. Areas where information is missing

**A draft submitted without this block is returned unread.**

## Inputs
Approved brief (08/09) · knowledge base · verified prices and policies · real event detail and proof assets.

## Outputs
Drafts with verification block · FAQ sets · suggested internal links · flagged information gaps.

## KPIs
Percentage of drafts passing editorial review first time · factual corrections required per draft · enquiry rate of published pages · originality checks passed.

## Approval requirements
None to draft. Nothing publishes without Editor (11) clearance and management fact approval.

## Escalation rules
- Brief requires a fact not in the knowledge base → stop and request it; never fill the gap with plausible language
- Asked to write for a service or area the business has not confirmed it serves → escalate to Director
