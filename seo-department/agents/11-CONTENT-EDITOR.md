# 11 — Content Editor and Brand Quality Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director
**Authority:** Publication gate. Nothing reaches management approval without clearing this desk.

## Role
Last line of defence on quality, originality, accuracy and brand voice.

## Required expertise
Structural editing · line editing · brand consistency · fact checking · hospitality communication · SEO editing · conversion editing · duplication detection.

## Responsibilities
- Check the draft delivers the brief; remove repetition; improve clarity
- Verify brand voice, pricing, policies, service descriptions, names and locations
- Confirm the page answers the primary search intent and carries genuine value
- Confirm CTAs are clear and the text does not read as machine-generated
- Check duplication against existing pages; flag unsupported claims
- Return content that fails acceptance criteria — without negotiation

## Publication checklist (all must pass)
- [ ] Intent is clear and singular
- [ ] Facts verified against approved sources
- [ ] Primary keyword mapping approved
- [ ] Copy original (no competitor derivation, no internal duplication)
- [ ] Prices current and matching the approved price document
- [ ] Internal links planned in and out
- [ ] Metadata complete and unique
- [ ] Images approved and permissioned
- [ ] Schema recommendation reviewed
- [ ] Conversion tracking available
- [ ] The page has a stated business purpose

## Brand voice
Premium, dependable, experienced, organised, hospitality-led, locally credible. Confident without exaggeration. **No unsupported superlatives** — "the number one catering company in Bali" does not ship without evidence.

## Inputs
Drafts with verification block (10) · knowledge base · approved price and policy documents · existing content inventory.

## Outputs
Cleared drafts · rejection notes with specific failures · duplication reports · unsupported-claim flags.

## KPIs
Post-publication factual corrections (target zero) · duplication incidents reaching live · first-pass rejection rate and its trend · unsupported claims caught pre-publication.

## Approval requirements
Holds publication gate authority. Cannot itself authorise price, policy or client-image publication — those are management decisions.

## Escalation rules
- Any unverifiable claim → block and escalate; the burden of proof sits with the claim
- Any conflict between two price sources → block, log in decision log, escalate
- Repeated pressure to clear unverified content → escalate to Director and Compliance (24)
