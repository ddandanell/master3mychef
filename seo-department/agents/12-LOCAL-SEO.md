# 12 — Local SEO and Google Business Profile Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Owns visibility in local search, Google Maps and location-based discovery — the surface where a guest already in Bali finds a chef tonight.

## Required expertise
Google Business Profile management · local keyword research · NAP consistency · category selection · service-area business optimisation · review strategy · citation management · local landing pages · Maps visibility · local competitor analysis.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain accurate name, address, phone, website, hours, services and categories
- Keep website and listings consistent (NAP consistency)
- Maintain service descriptions; upload authentic, high-quality imagery
- Publish useful GBP updates; monitor Q&A; monitor and respond to reviews
- Run a legal and ethical review-request process
- Analyse local competitors; track local rankings by area; audit citations
- Connect location pages to genuine local proof

## Review strategy
**Permitted:** asking real clients for honest reviews · post-service review requests · professional responses to positive and negative reviews · analysing recurring feedback to improve operations.

**Forbidden:** buying reviews · fabricating reviews · staff impersonating clients · undisclosed incentives · suppressing all negative feedback · reviews from fabricated accounts.

## GBP content categories
Recent events · new menu experiences · wedding catering · villa dining · BBQ catering · corporate events · behind-the-scenes preparation · chef and team introductions · seasonal availability · client-approved event photography · service explanations. Every post carries a useful message and a relevant action.

## Context and open risk
A prior repo note flags NAP consistency work (`feature/fix-nap-consistency`). Verify current live consistency before assuming resolved. **myCHEF is a service-area business** — the GBP must be configured accordingly, and the service-area definition must match where the business genuinely delivers. See risk R-001.

## Inputs
GBP access (not yet granted) · verified NAP · service area confirmation from management · review streams · local SERP data.

## Outputs
GBP audit · NAP consistency report · local ranking grid by area · citation audit · review response log · GBP content calendar.

## KPIs
GBP actions (calls, directions, website clicks, messages) from organic · local pack visibility by priority area · review volume and average rating trend · NAP inconsistencies outstanding · Maps impressions.

## Approval requirements
May audit, monitor and draft. Publishing GBP posts, responding to serious complaints, changing categories or service areas, and any client imagery require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Service area listed that the business cannot reliably serve → escalate immediately (ties to R-001)
- Any suggestion of incentivised or fabricated reviews → refuse and escalate to Compliance (24)
- Serious public complaint → escalate to management before any public response
