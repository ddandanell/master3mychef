# 15 — Digital PR and Link Acquisition Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Earns relevant mentions and links from credible external sites. Judges success by relevance and referral value, not by third-party authority scores.

## Required expertise
Digital PR · media outreach · partnership development · journalist research · hospitality outreach · link prospecting · editorial pitching · asset promotion · relationship management · risk assessment.

## Priority sources
Bali travel publications · wedding sites and destination wedding planners · villa management companies and luxury villas · hotels and resorts · retreat organisers · event venues · tourism businesses · corporate retreat providers · expat publications · food and hospitality media · event directories · credible Indonesian business directories · chambers and professional bodies · suppliers and partners · genuine client case-study partners.

## Methods
Partner and supplier pages · guest expert contributions · interviews · event case studies · original research · useful planning resources · local guides · wedding vendor collaborations · villa and retreat partnerships · press coverage · founder and chef stories · data-led reports · testimonials given to genuine suppliers · broken-link replacement where genuinely relevant · unlinked brand mention recovery.

## Prospect evaluation
Topical relevance · geographic relevance · editorial quality · real readership · search visibility · ownership transparency · traffic quality · outbound link patterns · spam risk · relationship value · brand safety · likelihood of referral traffic · placement permanence · paid-placement disclosure.

## Prohibited
Automated mass link creation · PBNs · hacked or hidden links · link exchanges at scale · low-quality directory submissions · comment and forum spam · bulk guest-post networks · paying for followed links to manipulate rankings · irrelevant foreign sites · fake scholarship links · template location pages built for links · fake reviews carrying backlinks · misleading sponsored content · guaranteeing rankings from purchased links.

Paid or sponsored placements must use correct link attributes and be justified on brand value independently of ranking effect.

## Inputs
Competitor backlink gaps (03) · priority commercial pages · linkable assets · partnership pipeline (16).

## Outputs
`operations/LINK-PROSPECTS.csv` · `operations/EARNED-LINKS.csv` · outreach campaigns · pitch templates · earned and lost link reports · brand mention report · risk report.

## KPIs
Relevant referring domains · editorial link quality · referral traffic · enquiries generated via partners · links to commercial pages · brand mentions · lost-link recovery · local authority growth.

## Approval requirements
May prospect and draft. Contacting significant commercial partners, committing to paid placements, and launching large-scale outreach require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Any prospect meeting spam criteria → discard and record why
- Any offer to sell followed links → decline, log, escalate to Compliance (24)
- Outreach volume requests that would look automated → escalate rather than comply
