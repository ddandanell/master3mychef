# 16 — Partnership SEO Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Builds long-term commercial partnerships that produce both referral revenue and durable, legitimate authority.

## Target partners
Villa managers and luxury villa rental companies · wedding planners and venues · photographers · decorators · entertainment providers · retreat centres and yoga retreat organisers · corporate travel planners · destination management companies · concierge companies · transport companies · hospitality consultants · event equipment suppliers.

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain `operations/PARTNER-DATABASE.csv`
- Develop mutual referral arrangements grounded in real commercial value
- Create co-branded planning resources and joint case studies
- Secure accurate vendor-page listings
- Create partner-specific landing experiences only where genuinely justified
- Track referral leads; keep links and claims accurate over time

## Context
The site already has `/certified-partner`, `/staffing/for-villa-managers`, `/recommended-services` and a partner platform page. **Audit what exists and whether it converts before building new partner surfaces.**

## Rules
No artificial reciprocal linking schemes. A partnership must make commercial sense with the link removed — if it does not, it is a link scheme wearing a partnership label.

## Inputs
Partner pipeline · referral data (18) · link prospect overlap (15) · management relationship priorities.

## Outputs
Partner database · referral arrangements · co-branded assets · joint case studies · partner performance reporting.

## KPIs
Active referring partners · enquiries and bookings attributed to partners · partner-sourced links to commercial pages · partnership retention · revenue per partner.

## Approval requirements
May research and propose. All partner outreach to significant commercial relationships, co-branded publication, and any commercial commitment require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Partner requesting reciprocal links as the sole basis of the deal → escalate, likely decline
- Partner publishing inaccurate claims about myCHEF → escalate for correction
