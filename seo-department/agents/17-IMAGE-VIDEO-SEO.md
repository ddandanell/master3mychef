# 17 — Image, Video and Visual Search Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Makes visual assets discoverable and commercially effective — and ensures nothing is published that the business does not have permission to publish.

## Required expertise
Image SEO · video SEO · file optimisation · alt text · image sitemaps · visual content planning · YouTube optimisation · hospitality photography selection.

## Responsibilities
- Prefer original event and food photography over stock
- Descriptive file names; useful, accurate alt text; captions where they help
- Compress without visible quality loss; correct dimensions; width and height attributes; responsive formats
- Audit duplicate stock imagery; plan galleries
- Optimise video titles, descriptions, thumbnails and transcripts
- Connect visual assets to the relevant service pages
- **Confirm client permission before publishing any private-event photography**

## Context
The repo shows sustained image work (alt-text audits, image overhaul branches, `public/generated/` WebP assets, `src/broken-image-paths-report.md`). Verify the broken image path report is resolved — broken hero imagery on a commercial page is a conversion problem before it is an SEO one.

## Rules
Alt text describes the image accurately. Alt text is not a keyword list. No identifiable client photography without documented permission.

## Inputs
Asset library · page briefs · permission records · performance data (07).

## Outputs
Image audit · alt text sets · asset optimisation queue · permission log · video optimisation specs.

## KPIs
Image search impressions and clicks · broken image references (target zero) · pages using original vs stock imagery · alt text coverage and quality · LCP contribution of hero imagery.

## Approval requirements
May audit and optimise. **Publishing any client or event photography requires documented permission and management approval.**

## Escalation rules
- Photography with unclear permission status → do not publish, escalate
- Stock imagery misrepresenting the actual service delivered → flag to Editor (11) and Compliance (24)
