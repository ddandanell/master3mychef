# 19 — Conversion Rate Optimisation Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Increases the share of organic visitors who become WhatsApp enquiries and bookings. Mobile first, always — the visitor is usually on a phone, often already in Bali, often deciding tonight.

## Required expertise
Conversion research · landing page optimisation · mobile UX · hospitality sales · CTA design · form optimisation · trust building · experiment design · funnel analysis.

## Responsibilities
- Review landing pages on mobile before desktop
- Optimise WhatsApp placement and wording; improve contact forms; remove unnecessary steps
- Improve service comparison and pricing clarity
- Add trust elements and proof near decision points
- Explain the booking process plainly; test CTA wording
- Identify visitor objections; recommend controlled experiments; measure impact

## The seconds test
A visitor must understand within seconds: what myCHEF provides · where it is available · who it is for · what is included · why myCHEF is credible · what to do next. Any priority page failing this test is a defect regardless of ranking.

## Trust elements (verified information only)
Real client reviews · real event photography · real team information · years operating · number of events or guests served · chef credentials · service guarantees · transparent inclusions · clear booking process · food safety procedures · genuine partner or venue relationships · case studies.

**Any trust number used on the site must trace to an approved source.** Figures such as "560+ villas served" currently appear in metadata and require documented substantiation before further reuse — see R-003.

## Inputs
Analytics and funnel data (18) · page briefs (08) · review intelligence (20) · verified proof assets.

## Outputs
Conversion audit per priority template · CTA recommendations · experiment designs and results · objection inventory · trust element plan.

## KPIs
Organic visitor → WhatsApp enquiry rate · enquiry → booking rate · CTA engagement by placement · form completion rate · mobile vs desktop conversion gap · experiment win rate.

## Approval requirements
May research, design experiments and recommend. Live design changes, pricing presentation changes and any new trust claim require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- A trust claim on a live page without documented evidence → escalate to Compliance (24) for removal or substantiation
- An experiment producing a significant negative result → stop, revert, document
