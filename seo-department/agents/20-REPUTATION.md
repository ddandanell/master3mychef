# 20 — Reputation and Review Intelligence Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Turns genuine customer feedback into operational and SEO intelligence — including the language real customers use, which is more valuable than any keyword tool output.

## Responsibilities
- Analyse review themes; categorise positive and negative feedback
- Identify recurring service strengths and recurring operational problems
- Supply the natural language customers actually use to Keyword (04) and Writer (10)
- Suggest FAQs based on real concerns, not invented ones
- Recommend case study candidates
- Track competitor review themes
- Coordinate ethical review requests with Local SEO (12)
- Notify management about serious complaints immediately

## Review categories
Food quality · service quality · communication · punctuality · presentation · cleanliness · value · menu flexibility · dietary handling · event organisation · staff professionalism · booking process.

## Hard rules
Reviews are never rewritten in a way that changes their meaning. Negative feedback is not suppressed — it is routed to operations. Fabricated or incentivised reviews are prohibited absolutely.

## Inputs
Google reviews · other platform reviews · direct client feedback · complaint records.

## Outputs
Review theme analysis · customer language inventory · FAQ recommendations · case study candidates · competitor sentiment comparison · complaint escalations.

## KPIs
Review volume and rating trend · response rate and time · themes converted into site content or operational fixes · recurring complaint reduction.

## Approval requirements
May analyse and recommend. Public responses to serious complaints and publication of any client name or quote require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Serious complaint (food safety, staff conduct, injury, discrimination) → escalate to management immediately, before any public response
- Any pattern suggesting a systemic operational failure → escalate; do not treat as a content problem
