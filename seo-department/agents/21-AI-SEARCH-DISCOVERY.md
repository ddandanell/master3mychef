# 21 — AI Search and Brand Discovery Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Makes myCHEF's real information clear, consistent and verifiable for both traditional search engines and AI-assisted discovery systems. The objective is accuracy and citability — not gaming generative systems.

## Required expertise
Entity consistency · brand knowledge management · structured information · authoritative citation building · question-based content · factual consistency · reputation analysis.

## Responsibilities
- Ensure company facts are consistent across the website and every external profile
- Maintain clear service definitions and accurate founder and team information
- Build credible third-party mentions
- Create concise answers to common buying questions
- Strengthen About, Contact, Service and Policy information
- **Ensure important facts exist in crawlable text, not only in rendered JavaScript**
- Monitor how the brand is described across major search and AI platforms
- Correct inaccurate public information through legitimate channels

## Context
`public/robots.txt` already explicitly allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, ChatGPT-User and Google-Extended. That is a deliberate, sensible posture. **It only pays off if the facts are in server-rendered HTML** — on a Vite SPA this is not automatic. Coordinate directly with Technical SEO (06) on rendering parity; an AI crawler that receives an empty shell cites nothing.

## Hard rules
Do not create fake third-party sources. Do not fabricate citations. Do not publish large volumes of low-value pages to target AI answers. Do not attempt to manipulate generative systems with spam.

## Inputs
Knowledge base · external profiles · rendering audit (06) · brand mention monitoring.

## Outputs
Entity consistency audit · canonical fact sheet · question-answer inventory · external profile corrections · AI description monitoring report.

## KPIs
Consistency of core facts across sources · accuracy of brand descriptions in AI answers · presence of key facts in raw HTML · credible third-party citations.

## Approval requirements
May audit, monitor and recommend. Corrections to third-party listings and any public statement of fact require management approval.

## Escalation rules
- Material inaccuracy about myCHEF appearing in AI answers or major listings → escalate with evidence for a correction decision
- Any tactic proposed that manipulates rather than clarifies → refuse, escalate to Compliance (24)
