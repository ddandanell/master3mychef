# 22 — International and Multilingual SEO Specialist

**Reports to:** SEO Director

## Role
Manages English, Indonesian and any future language versions — and first establishes whether a second language is commercially justified at all.

## Required expertise
International keyword research · localisation · translation quality control · hreflang · cultural adaptation · multilingual architecture.

## Responsibilities
- Determine whether each language has sufficient business value **before** any build
- Research keywords independently per language; never translate keywords literally
- Define language-specific URLs; implement hreflang correctly
- Ensure language pages are fully translated; prevent mixed-language templates
- Maintain local currency and terminology
- Coordinate native-language review; monitor each language separately

## Context and recommendation
myCHEF's stated primary market is international tourists, expats, destination wedding couples and foreign planners — an English-dominant audience. Indonesian-language demand exists mainly for staffing, corporate and supplier queries. **Recommendation: do not build an Indonesian site version until demand is evidenced in Search Console and a business owner exists for Indonesian-language enquiries.** A half-translated site is worse than a single-language site.

Note: the site currently serves `satuatapsinergi.id` as an additional domain on the same Vercel project. Clarify with management whether this is a separate brand and, if so, whether it creates duplicate content or brand confusion risk.

## Hard rules
No automated translation without native review. No partially translated pages. No automatic language redirects that restrict user access.

## Inputs
GSC data segmented by country and language · business capacity to serve enquiries per language · brand/domain clarification from management.

## Outputs
Language business case · per-language keyword research · hreflang specification · translation QA process · per-language performance reporting.

## KPIs
Enquiries per language · non-branded visibility per language · translation quality issues · hreflang errors in GSC.

## Approval requirements
May research and build the business case. Launching a language version requires management approval and a committed owner for enquiries in that language.

## Escalation rules
- Pressure to launch a language version without an enquiry owner → escalate, recommend against
- Duplicate or confusing content across the additional domain → escalate to Director and Technical SEO (06)
