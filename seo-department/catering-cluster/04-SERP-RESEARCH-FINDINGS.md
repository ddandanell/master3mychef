# Document 4 — Live SERP Research Findings

**Date:** 2026-07-28 · **Method:** direct Google SERP inspection (browser), not tool estimates
**Status:** these findings **contradict the assigned keyword ownership** and need a ruling before rewriting begins.

## Finding 1 — "catering Bali" is the wrong head term for myCHEF

**Live SERP for `catering bali` is almost entirely Indonesian-language domestic caterers:**

| Ranking site | Language | What they sell |
|---|---|---|
| bali-catering.com | Indonesian | Prasmanan, nasi kotak, rice bowl, snack box |
| tiacateringbali.com | Indonesian | Pernikahan, corporate, acara adat |
| damarbalicatering.com | Indonesian | Pernikahan, acara kantor, arisan, reuni |
| widaayucatering (IG) | Indonesian | Jaje Bali, snackbox, nasi yasa |
| Bridestory | Indonesian | Wedding vendor directory |
| baguscateringbali.com | English/ID | Denpasar-based, weddings + corporate |

**"People also search for":** Catering bali price · Catering bali menu · Catering bali packages · **Catering harian bali** (daily catering) · **Catering prasmanan Bali** (traditional buffet) · **Catering Bali murah** (cheap catering).

**Interpretation.** This is a *domestic, Indonesian-language, price-sensitive* market — boxed rice, traditional buffets, arisan and office catering. "Murah" (cheap) is a top related search. That buyer is not myCHEF's customer, and the price gap is roughly an order of magnitude.

**Consequence.** Optimising `/catering` hard for "catering Bali" would mean competing with established domestic caterers for enquiries myCHEF would have to decline. High volume, wrong intent, low qualification. This is the clearest example yet of why volume alone must never drive priority.

## Recommended change to keyword ownership

The assigned map gives `/catering` the head term "catering Bali" and `/catering/villa-catering` the term "villa catering Bali". **The SERP evidence suggests inverting the emphasis:**

| URL | Assigned | Recommended | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/catering` | catering Bali | **catering Bali (villa & event focus)** — qualify the term, do not chase the generic domestic query | Generic term attracts unqualified domestic demand |
| `/catering/villa-catering` | villa catering Bali | **villa catering Bali** — treat as the commercial head term for the whole cluster | Right buyer, right price point, already ranking #3 |

`/catering` remains the hub and still needs to rank — but for **qualified** variants ("catering for villas Bali", "event catering Bali", "private catering Bali"), not the domestic generic.

## Competitor patterns worth matching

Observed on the page-1 sites for villa catering:
- Explicit **area naming in the title/description** (Canggu, Seminyak, Uluwatu, Ubud) — villa-catering-bali.online does this in its meta description
- **Private chef + catering framing combined**, not separated — several competitors sell both on one page
- **Direct WhatsApp number in the SERP description** (Bali Prime Catering) — visible contact in the snippet
- **Local pack presence** — three caterers hold Maps positions on this query

Not observed, and therefore an opening: none of the page-1 competitors publish transparent per-person pricing in their snippet. myCHEF's new hero price line is a genuine differentiator here.

---

## Confidence and limits

- These are **live SERP observations from one location and one session**, not volume data. Semrush units are exhausted and Search Console is not granted, so no impression or volume figures support this.
- SERPs are personalised and geo-dependent; results seen from a different location may differ.
- Findings 1 and 2 are strong because they rest on the *language and business model* of the ranking sites, which is not a subtle signal — but they should still be confirmed against Search Console query data before large-scale rewriting.

## Blocking question for management

**Do you want `/catering` to chase "catering Bali" (high volume, largely domestic Indonesian buyers, low qualification), or to own the qualified international villa/event segment where myCHEF already ranks and the economics work?**

Everything downstream — titles, H1s, internal anchors, and which pages get rewritten first — depends on that answer.
