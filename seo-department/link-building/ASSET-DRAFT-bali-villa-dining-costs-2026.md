### The rules that surprise people (good FAQ/citation material)
- No hidden totals: quotes state one fixed figure before you commit.
- Tax and service are disclosed in prose (11% + 10%); displayed figures are selling prices.
- Dietary requirements (vegan, GF, halal, allergies, kids) at no extra charge.
- General minimum is 5 guests; only babi guling (6, whole-pig portioning) and buffet (30, setup economics) differ.

## Why this earns links
1. **It's real data in a market that hides prices.** Bali villa/wedding media constantly writes "how much does X cost" content with no primary source to cite.

3. **Villa managers** can link it as a guest resource instead of answering price questions themselves.
