## NOT changed — needs a decision

### 1. Weekend hours look wrong, and may be costing enquiries

The GBP says the business is open **Saturday 08:00–10:00** and **Sunday 09:00–12:00**. For a private-chef and catering business whose product is villa dinners, that is almost certainly a data-entry error — and it is the kind that quietly costs money:

- Someone searching "private chef Bali" on a Saturday afternoon sees **"Closed"** on the listing.
- Saturday and Sunday evening are when villa dinners actually happen.
- Wednesday closing at 23:30 while every other weekday closes at 22:00 also looks accidental.

The site currently declares `Mo-Su 07:00-22:00`, which contradicts the GBP on the opening hour too (07:00 vs 08:00).

**I did not copy the GBP hours into the site**, because publishing a two-hour Saturday window in structured data would spread the error rather than fix it. The fix belongs on the GBP first.

**Action for the owner:** correct the hours on the Google Business Profile, tell me the real ones, and I will mirror them into `siteFacts` and the `openingHours` field in one pass.

### 2. Street address is on the site but not on the GBP

The site publishes `Jl. Tukad Barito Timur III No.16, Panjer, Denpasar Selatan`. The GBP shows only the sub-district — typical of a service-area business that hides its address.

Not necessarily wrong; a real office can be published while the listing stays service-area. But it is an unresolved NAP difference. **Decide:** either add the street address to the GBP so both match, or accept the difference deliberately and record it here.

### 3. Reviews: 5.0 from 8 — do not mark this up

There is now a real, verifiable rating where previously there was none. `aggregateRatingSchema()` in `SeoHead.tsx` was deliberately stubbed out to return `null` after Search Console flagged it invalid.

**Leave it that way.** Google does not allow a business to publish `AggregateRating` markup about itself — self-serving review markup is ineligible for rich results and risks a manual action. The correct route to showing those stars in search is the GBP itself, which already carries them. The site's `sameAs` link to the profile is what connects the two, and that is in place.

The old open item "reviewCount unknown" can be closed: the count is 8, and the answer is that we still do not mark it up.

## Why this matters

Nine of your top ten Search Console queries are local private-chef terms. For those, Google leans on how confidently it can tie this website to that Maps listing. Before this branch the site gave it a different name, a pin 1.2 km away, a phone in three formats and six address strings. That is now one name, one pin, one phone and one address.

The remaining gaps — hours and street address — are on the GBP side, not the code side.
