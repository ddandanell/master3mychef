# Growth Decision — how to win both categories

**Decided:** 2026-07-28 · **Decided by:** SEO Director, on owner delegation
**Basis:** GSC export (762 queries, 162 pages, 28 days), Ahrefs DR 23, live SERP research

---

## Decision 1 — The homepage owns "private chef bali". Do not build a new page for it.

**Evidence:**

| Page | Impressions | Clicks | Position |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `/` | **5,934** | 225 | 9.3 |
| next largest (`/locations/canggu`) | 1,050 | 31 | 14.9 |

The homepage carries **more impressions than the next five pages combined**, already titles on *Private Chef Bali*, and holds the site's link equity.

**Why not a dedicated `/private-chef-bali` page:** at **DR 23** you cannot afford to build authority twice. A new page starts at zero, needs internal links and months of ageing, and would compete with the homepage for the identical intent — manufacturing exactly the cannibalisation this department has spent the day removing. The homepage is not failing to target the term; it is trying to serve every intent at once and averaging position 9.3 across all of them.

**The fix is focus, not a new page.** The homepage should sell *private chef in Bali* above the fold and route every other intent — catering, fine dining, staffing, events — to its own page through clear links rather than competing copy.

---

## Decision 2 — Hub and spoke, with authority flowing one direction

At DR 23, authority is the binding constraint. It must be concentrated, not spread.

```
                    /  (homepage)
            owns "private chef bali"
                        │
        ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
        │               │               │
 /private-chef/{area}   /catering    /fine-dining
 owns "private chef     owns format   owns tasting/
 {area}" — 61 pages     terms         occasion terms
 ALREADY WINNING
        ▲
        │ links up
 /locations/{area}
 informational — feeds the commercial page, never competes
```

**Rule:** informational pages feed commercial pages. Commercial pages feed the homepage's topical authority. Nothing points sideways into a competing page.

## Decision 3 — The biggest single structural win: route `/locations/*` traffic to `/private-chef/*`

| Page | Impressions | Position | Job |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `/locations/canggu` | **1,050** | 14.9 | informational dining guide |
| `/private-chef/canggu` | (ranks 5.9 for the commercial query) | 5.9 | **commercial** |

The *informational* page collects the impressions; the *commercial* page converts. Across the location family this is likely several thousand impressions arriving on pages that do not sell.

**Action:** every `/locations/{area}` page carries a prominent, contextual link to `/private-chef/{area}` for the same area. No consolidation, no deletion — both pages keep their distinct job, and the traffic gets a route to the enquiry.

This is the highest-leverage change available that requires no new content and no authority.

---

## Decision 4 — Grow catering *through* private chef, not alongside it

**Evidence:** chef queries **57.8%** of impressions, catering **25%**. `/catering` sits at **position 27.9** — page three. It does not rank and will not rank quickly by direct assault at DR 23.

**Do not** invest in `/catering` as an independent ranking target yet. **Do** treat it as the conversion path for traffic private chef already earns: a guest arriving for a private chef discovers BBQ, buffet or grazing when they realise they are hosting 30 people, not 6.

The BBQ page proves the model — `/catering/bbq-catering` already holds **690 impressions at position 10.4**, far better than the hub. Format pages can rank; the generic hub cannot. Feed the formats from the chef pages.

**Sequencing:** private chef first, catering second — but the same work grows both, because the internal links that route chef traffic into catering formats lift both categories at once. That is how the two grow together rather than competing for the same effort.

## Priority order

| # | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sharpen the homepage on *private chef Bali*; route other intents out via links | 5,934 impressions, position 9.3 — the single biggest lever |
| 2 | Link every `/locations/{area}` → `/private-chef/{area}` | ~thousands of impressions currently landing on non-selling pages |
| 3 | Fix the four page-two queries earning **zero** clicks | `bali villa catering`, `private chef bali villa`, `private chef villa menu`, `corporate event catering bali` |
| 4 | Snippet fixes on page-one, near-zero-CTR queries | `private chef` (8.2), `home chef` (9.5) — cheap wins |
| 5 | Internal links from chef pages into catering **format** pages | Grows catering without fighting for the hub |
| 6 | Begin link building | DR 23 is the ceiling on everything above |

---

## What this decision deliberately rejects

- **A new `/private-chef-bali` page** — would split authority the site cannot spare
- **Consolidating the 61 area pages** — they are the proof the model works (Canggu 5.9, Uluwatu 6.3)
- **Direct assault on `/catering` at position 27.9** — wrong fight at this domain rating
- **More content volume** — the pages already average well over 2,000 words; position, not length, is the constraint

## Honest expectation

First position across *all* Bali areas for both categories is a **6–12 month objective at DR 23**, not a quarter. Items 1–4 should show movement within 4–8 weeks because they work with rankings that already exist. Item 6 determines whether the ceiling ever lifts.
