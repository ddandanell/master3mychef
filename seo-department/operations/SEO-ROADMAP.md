## Strategic sequence

The site is not starting from zero. It has 248 indexed URLs, 187 page components, a centralised metadata layer and a substantial editorial library. **The constraint is not content volume — it is measurement, intent clarity and conversion.** The roadmap reflects that: consolidate and measure before publishing more.

## Days 16–30 — Critical fixes

Priority order is severity, not convenience:

1. Rendering/indexation defects (if T-001 confirms a problem, everything else waits)
2. Conversion tracking (without it, nothing after this point is measurable)
3. Service-area accuracy — unpublish or qualify areas not genuinely served
4. Pricing accuracy across the 4+ URLs carrying cost information
5. Wedding cluster cannibalisation (C-004 — four assets, one intent, highest booking value)
6. Mobile usability and speed defects on priority commercial templates

## First-year priorities

1. **Own the core category** — private chef Bali, catering Bali, villa catering Bali, event catering Bali
2. **Build service depth** — distinct, authoritative pages for real high-value services
3. **Build local relevance** — Bali-wide strength; location pages only where justified
4. **Build proof** — case studies, genuine reviews, galleries, team profiles, real operational detail
5. **Build authority** — relevant mentions from Bali tourism, villa, wedding and retreat organisations
6. **Improve conversion** — turn visibility into WhatsApp conversations
7. **Build measurement culture** — every action carries a hypothesis, a date and a result

---

## What this roadmap will not do

- Publish new pages before the existing 248 are assessed
- Report ranking movement as commercial success
- Create location pages for areas the business cannot serve
- Chase volume keywords that do not convert
