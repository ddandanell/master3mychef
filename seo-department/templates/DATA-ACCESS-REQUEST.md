Nothing below is a nice-to-have. Each entry states what is needed, why, what will be analysed, and which decision it unblocks.

### 1. Google Search Console
- **Why:** the only source of true query, impression, click and index data
- **Analysis:** query-per-URL cannibalisation (R-002), non-branded visibility, index coverage, priority page performance
- **Unblocks:** wedding cluster consolidation (TASK-004), content inventory decisions (TASK-008), the entire keyword priority model
- **Access:** Full user for the SEO account

### 2. Google Analytics 4
- **Why:** connects visibility to enquiries
- **Analysis:** landing page conversion, WhatsApp click events, device and geography splits, funnel drop-off
- **Unblocks:** all conversion reporting; any claim about commercial performance
- **Access:** Viewer minimum; Editor if tracking gaps require fixing

### 3. Google Tag Manager
- **Why:** to audit and repair conversion tracking (R-006)
- **Analysis:** whether WhatsApp clicks, form submits and calls actually fire
- **Unblocks:** measurement of the primary conversion action
- **Access:** Publish rights, or a developer contact who has them

### 4. Google Business Profile
- **Why:** local and Maps visibility is where in-destination guests convert
- **Analysis:** categories, service area, NAP consistency, reviews, GBP actions
- **Unblocks:** local strategy; also ties to service-area accuracy (R-001)
- **Access:** Manager

### 5. Backlink data (Semrush / Ahrefs)
- **Why:** authority baseline and competitor gap
- **Analysis:** referring domains, link quality, lost links, competitor gaps
- **Unblocks:** link strategy and realistic competitiveness assessment
- **Access:** existing Semrush connection may cover this — confirm seat

### 6. Booking / enquiry records
- **Why:** the department is measured on bookings, not clicks
- **Analysis:** enquiry-to-booking rate, booking value by service and area, seasonality
- **Unblocks:** commercial reporting and correct prioritisation by profit rather than volume
- **Access:** aggregate export is sufficient — **no personal customer data required or wanted**
