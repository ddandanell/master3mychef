## Notes

- Headlines prioritize "you" and "your villa" over "we" to center the guest experience.
- "Michelin-trained" is woven subtly (Options 2, 4) to signal pedigree without pretension.
- Pricing anchor (IDR 1.5M) sets expectation without being the lead message.
- Body copy maintains 2:1 "you" to "we" ratio per brand voice rules.
- All copy references the villa lifestyle specifically — pool, sunset, venue — not generic "home."
