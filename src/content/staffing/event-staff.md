## How Many Event Staff Do You Need?

Staffing ratios depend on event format, venue layout, and service style. The following is how we plan event teams for our clients, not a rigid formula.

**Seated Dinner**

- Formal plated service: 1 waiter per 8–10 guests
- Family-style service: 1 waiter per 12–15 guests
- Dedicated wine or beverage server: 1 per 20 guests
- Kitchen runners: recommended for villas with non-commercial kitchens or outdoor cooking setups

**Cocktail Reception or Standing Party**

- Tray-passed canapés: 1 service staff per 15–20 guests
- Standard bartender: 1 per 30–40 guests
- Mixology bartender: 1 per 25–30 guests
- Host or greeter staff: recommended for arrivals and guest flow

**Wedding (Multi-Phase)**

- Ceremony: minimal support, usually 1–2 staff for water, programs, or guest comfort
- Cocktail hour: 1 service staff per 15 guests, 1 bartender per 30–40 guests
- Reception: 1 waiter per 8–10 guests, dedicated beverage service, kitchen runners
- Breakdown crew: separate from service staff to allow efficient close without fatiguing the guest-facing team

**Corporate Retreats & Wellness Events**

- Meal functions: typically 1 service staff per 15–20 guests
- Breakfast or buffet brunch: 1 per 20–25 guests
- Non-alcoholic beverage specialists available for wellness-focused events

Villa events often require more staff than hotel events because the venue lacks built-in infrastructure. There are no commercial kitchens, no service lifts, and no dedicated back-of-house paths. Staff must navigate stairs, garden paths, pool decks, and sand. This is why villa-specific experience matters, and why we plan logistics before the event day.

## Vetting & Presentation Standards

Every myCHEF.id event staff member completes our documented verification protocol before joining an event. We do not send strangers to your villa or wedding venue.

1. **Identity Verification.** Government-issued ID (KTP) is reviewed and confirmed.
2. **Work History Verification.** Previous hospitality employers are contacted directly to confirm roles, dates, and responsibilities.
3. **Reference & Experience Checks.** We obtain a minimum of two professional references from hospitality supervisors or event coordinators.
4. **Local Police Record Review (SKCK).** We require a current Surat Keterangan Catatan Kepolisian from the candidate's local police district, confirming no active criminal proceedings. SKCK is district-level and valid for six months.
5. **Skills Assessment.** Candidates demonstrate tray carrying, drink pouring, guest interaction, and villa-environment service.
6. **Presentation & Grooming Standard.** Uniform fitting, hygiene briefing, and professional appearance review.
7. **Event-Specific Training.** Villa protocols include working with private chefs, navigating residential spaces, discreet service, and guest interaction standards.

All staff are provided with event-appropriate attire: clean black trousers, white or black service shirts, and non-slip footwear suitable for villa surfaces. For formal weddings, we can adjust attire to match the event dress code. For brand activations, we can incorporate your brand colors or provide branded elements.

## FAQ

**Q1: How far in advance do I need to book event staff?**

For weddings and large events with 50 or more guests, we recommend booking 2–4 weeks in advance. For smaller villa parties and last-minute needs, we can often accommodate within 48–72 hours depending on availability. Earlier bookings allow us to plan staffing ratios, brief your team, and coordinate venue logistics more thoroughly.

**Q2: What happens if a staff member is unavailable on the event day?**

We confirm all staff 48 hours and 24 hours before your event, and we maintain a standby pool of pre-briefed backup staff for every role. If a team member becomes unavailable, your dedicated event coordinator activates a replacement who is already briefed on your event details and venue.

**Q3: How much does event staff cost in Bali?**

Event staffing costs depend on the number of guests, event type, service duration, staff roles, and whether you need specialized skills such as mixology. A seated dinner for 30 guests requires a different team than a 100-guest wedding or a multi-day corporate retreat. Contact us with your event details and we will provide a detailed, transparent quote with no hidden fees.

**Q4: Are your event staff trained for villa environments, or only hotels?**

Our event staff are specifically trained for villa and private event environments. Villa events have no commercial kitchen, no service corridors, and no built-in bar. Our staff know how to navigate pool decks, garden terraces, narrow stairs, and residential kitchens while working alongside private chefs and catering teams.

**Q5: Can I request specific staff or meet them before my event?**

For recurring clients and large events, we can arrange a brief introduction with your event coordinator and key team members. For most events, we select staff based on your event type and our knowledge of their experience and strengths.

**Q6: What do your staff wear? Can the uniform match my event theme?**

All event staff wear clean, professional uniforms: black trousers, white or black service shirts, and non-slip footwear. For formal weddings, we can provide additional formal attire elements. For branded corporate events, we can incorporate brand colors or provide branded aprons. Discuss attire details during your planning call.

**Q7: Do you provide bartenders who can make craft cocktails?**

Yes. We provide both standard bartenders for beer, wine, spirits, and basic cocktails, and mixology bartenders for craft cocktails, signature drinks, and presentation-focused service. If your event features a specific cocktail menu, we match a bartender with the appropriate skills and brief them on your list.

**Q8: What is included in your event staffing service?**

Your quote includes vetted and trained staff for the confirmed roles, uniformed presentation, pre-event briefing and coordination, event-day management by your dedicated coordinator, and backup staff on standby. We handle staff scheduling, travel logistics, and on-site coordination so you can focus on your event.

**Q9: Can you staff events outside of Bali, like the Gili Islands or Nusa Lembongan?**

Yes, we staff events across the Nusa Islands, Gili Islands, and Lombok for established clients. Island events require additional logistics planning, including ferry schedules and overnight accommodation for staff. We include all logistics in our plan and quote. For island events, we recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks in advance.

**Q10: Do you handle setup and breakdown, or just service?**

We can provide setup and breakdown crew as part of your staffing plan. Setup includes table and chair placement, décor support, bar setup, and venue preparation. Breakdown includes efficient clearing, equipment packing, and venue restoration. For weddings, we typically separate setup crew from service staff so the guest-facing team remains fresh and focused.

**Q11: What if my event runs over time?**

We build a confirmed service window into your quote, including setup and breakdown time. If your event runs beyond the confirmed window, overtime is charged at a pre-agreed rate. To avoid surprises, we recommend building a small buffer into your timeline. Your event coordinator will communicate clearly if overtime is approaching.

**Q12: How do I book event staff for my wedding or villa party?**

The easiest way is to WhatsApp us your event details: date, venue, guest count, event type, and any special requirements. We respond within a few hours with a recommended staffing plan and transparent quote. For large weddings and complex events, we may schedule a brief call to discuss logistics, timing, and service style. Once you confirm, we lock in your team and begin the briefing process.
