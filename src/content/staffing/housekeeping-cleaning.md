## Housekeeper Bali: Roles, Responsibilities, and Villa Standards

A professional villa housekeeper is not a "cleaning lady." In a well-run Bali villa, the housekeeper is a hospitality professional who protects the property's presentation, the guest experience, and often the fabric of the building itself.

**Daily villa cleaning**
All living spaces, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, and outdoor areas are maintained to hospitality standards: dust-free surfaces, streak-free mirrors, precisely arranged amenities, and floors that pass the barefoot test. We clean around guest schedules, not through them.

**Laundry and linen management**
One of the most valued capabilities in Bali. Our team handles bed-linen changes, towel laundering and folding, personal clothing ironing, delicate-fabric care, and seasonal storage. In luxury villas, linen care includes knowing which detergents suit which fabrics and how presentation matches the villa's aesthetic.

**Kitchen support and basic food preparation**
Many housekeepers also assist with kitchen organization, basic prep, grocery receipt and storage, refrigerator management, and pantry inventory. Where matched, we can place a housekeeper with cooking skills — though this complements, rather than replaces, a dedicated private chef.

**Guest discretion and privacy**
Your villa is a private sanctuary. Our housekeepers are briefed on privacy boundaries, professional interaction, and cultural sensitivity. Discretion is not optional; it is part of the role.

**Property care and reporting**
A good housekeeper notices problems before they become costly: a leaking tap, early mold, pest signs, or wear on linens. They report inventory needs and flag maintenance issues so your villa manager or our [maintenance support team](/staffing/maintenance) can act early.

**Flexible arrangements**
We match the arrangement to the villa: daily housekeepers for occupied villas, part-time support for smaller properties, live-in staff for full-service estates, and turnover cleaning specialists for short-term rentals.

## How We Verify Every Housekeeper Before They Enter Your Villa

Trust is built through visible process, not marketing claims. Every myCHEF housekeeper completes our documented seven-step verification protocol before their first placement.

**1. Identity verification**
We confirm every candidate's identity through their government-issued Indonesian ID (KTP) and cross-check that the documentation matches their application, work history, and references.

**2. Work history validation**
We contact previous employers directly to confirm dates, responsibilities, and reasons for departure. We look specifically for villa, hotel, or private household experience and ask what matters: Was she reliable? Did she understand hospitality standards? Why did she leave?

**3. Professional reference checks**
We conduct a minimum of two professional reference checks with previous supervisors or employers, by phone where possible. We ask directly about attendance, quality, trustworthiness, and ability to work with minimal supervision.

**4. Police clearance certificate (SKCK)**
We require a current SKCK from the candidate's local police district, confirming they are not currently involved in criminal proceedings there. We are transparent about its scope: SKCK is district-level, valid for six months, and obtained by the candidate directly. It is one important layer — not the only one.

**5. Practical skills assessment**
Every candidate completes a hands-on assessment: cleaning technique, linen folding and presentation, laundry-care knowledge, and attention-to-detail tests. We evaluate against documented competency standards, not impressions.

**6. Certification and health review**
Where applicable, we verify food-hygiene awareness, health fitness documentation, and any role-specific credentials. All verification is conducted with candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law.

**7. Supervised trial period**
Before full commitment, we offer a structured trial period with enhanced oversight and daily feedback. This lets you assess fit in your own villa before locking in a long-term arrangement.

## Daily, Part-Time, Live-In, or Turnover Cleaning — Which Do You Need?

The right arrangement depends on your villa size, occupancy, and lifestyle. We do not force every property into the same contract.

**Daily housekeeper**
The most common choice for occupied villas. Typically 6–8 hours, 5–6 days per week, maintaining cleaning standards, laundry, and guest readiness. Best for 3–6 bedroom villas, families with children, and properties where presentation matters every day.

**Part-time housekeeper**
A flexible option for smaller villas or owner-occupied properties. Usually 3–4 hours, 3–5 days per week, focusing on core cleaning and laundry. Best for 1–3 bedroom villas and owners who handle some daily tasks but need professional support.

**Live-in housekeeper**
For large villas and luxury estates requiring full-time presence. A live-in housekeeper resides on property, handles daily cleaning, turndown service, laundry, and can respond to guest needs outside normal hours. These arrangements include proper accommodation and clear employment terms.

**Turnover cleaning specialist**
For rental villas on Airbnb, Booking.com, or private rental channels. We provide rapid, thorough cleaning between guest check-out and check-in, including linen changes, amenity restocking, and quality inspection. Scheduling can integrate with your booking calendar.

Whichever arrangement you choose, myCHEF provides the vetted candidate, compliant employment contract, BPJS and THR administration, ongoing management, and replacement coverage.
