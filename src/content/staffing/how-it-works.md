## Section 2: Our Villa Staffing Process Bali

Transparency is what separates a premium staffing partner from a casual agency. Below is exactly what happens when you work with us.

### Step 1 — Consultation & Villa Service Profile

Every placement begins with a structured consultation. We learn about your property, your guest profile, your existing team, and the service culture you want to create. A six-bedroom estate in Uluwatu with international guests requires a different approach from a family villa in Sanur with young children. We ask about communication preferences, dietary requirements, language needs, and whether you are a hands-on owner or managing from another timezone.

**What this means for you:** You do not need to guess what roles you need. We help you define the right team structure before any recruitment begins.

### Step 2 — Talent Search & Network Activation

We do not post a generic advert and wait. We activate our existing network of hospitality professionals across Bali, including passive candidates who are currently employed but open to the right opportunity. For specialized roles such as a villa manager, private chef, or trilingual butler, we conduct targeted outreach. We also factor in geography: a candidate based in Kerobokan may be ideal for a Seminyak villa, while a Ubud-based professional may suit a quieter estate in Sidemen.

**What this means for you:** You meet two or three curated candidates, not two hundred random applications.

### Step 3 — Rigorous Vetting & Verification Protocol

This is the trust-critical stage. Every candidate completes our [documented recruitment and vetting protocol](/staffing/recruitment) before entering your home:

- Identity and work history verification via government-issued KTP and direct employer contact
- Reference and experience checks with a minimum of two previous hospitality supervisors
- Local police record review (SKCK) from the candidate's local district
- Practical skills assessment for the specific role
- Certification review, including food safety and hygiene credentials where applicable
- A supervised trial period with enhanced oversight and feedback loops

The protocol is conducted with full candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law.

**What this means for you:** You know exactly what has been checked before someone receives a key to your villa.

### Step 4 — Curated Shortlist & Interview Support

We present each matched candidate with a placement summary: verified experience, reference highlights, skills assessment results, and why we believe they fit your villa. We coordinate interviews at your property or via video call, and we provide guidance if you have never hired staff in Bali before. For most placements, we recommend a paid trial period of three to five days so you can observe the candidate in your actual environment.

**What this means for you:** You are not guessing in the interview. You have our assessment, the candidate's track record, and a trial to confirm fit.

### Step 5 — Contract, Compliance & Onboarding

Once you select your candidate, we handle the legal and administrative setup. This includes compliant employment contracts in Indonesian (PKWTT/PKWT), BPJS health and social insurance registration, THR holiday allowance structuring, and minimum wage compliance (UMK) for the relevant Bali regency. We act as the legal employer, which shields you from direct labor liability. Onboarding covers villa orientation, role-specific training, SOP introduction, and a WhatsApp communication group with your dedicated operations manager.

**What this means for you:** No legal ambiguity, no compliance anxiety, and no administrative burden on you.

### Step 6 — Ongoing Management & Replacement Support

This is where myCHEF.id differs from a basic recruitment agency. After placement, we stay involved. Your dedicated operations manager provides proactive attendance monitoring, manages coverage through a trained backup roster when staff are sick or on leave, conducts quarterly performance reviews, provides ongoing coaching, and acts as the communication bridge between you and your team. As the employer of record, we handle compliance and liability. If a placement does not work out, we provide a replacement within 30 days at no additional placement fee.

**What this means for you:** Your villa team is managed, not left to chance. Someone is always accountable.

## Section 8: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How does the myCHEF.id staffing process work from start to finish?**
We begin with a structured consultation to understand your villa, guests, and service needs. We then search our network, vet candidates through identity verification, reference checks, skills assessment, and current police clearance (SKCK), and present a curated shortlist of two to three matched candidates. After interviews and a trial period, we handle contracts, compliance, and onboarding — then provide ongoing management, attendance monitoring, and replacement support. Most standard placements take three to four weeks from first contact to an operational team member.

**Q2: How long does it take to find and place villa staff?**
Standard placements typically take three to four weeks, including consultation, vetting, interviews, and a trial period. For urgent needs, we can often place pre-vetted candidates from our standby pool within three to five days for standard roles such as housekeeping. Specialized roles such as villa managers or experienced chefs may take seven to ten days even for urgent placement.

**Q3: How do you vet and verify staff before placement?**
Every candidate completes our documented placement protocol: identity and work history verification via KTP and direct employer contact, reference and experience checks with previous supervisors, local police record review (SKCK), practical skills assessment, certification review, and a supervised trial period. This is conducted with full candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law.

**Q4: What happens if my staff member is sick or needs time off?**
We maintain a trained standby pool for every role. If your regular team member is unavailable, your dedicated operations manager activates a pre-briefed replacement with same-role experience, typically within the same service day. We also handle leave scheduling proactively so absences are planned, not surprises.

**Q5: What if the staff member does not work out after placement?**
We offer a 30-day replacement guarantee. If the placement is not the right fit, we source a replacement at no additional placement fee. We also conduct a structured review to understand what went wrong and refine our matching for future placements.

**Q6: Do you handle contracts, payroll, and legal compliance?**
Yes. We are the legal employer for all placed staff. We handle compliant employment contracts in Indonesian (PKWTT/PKWT), BPJS health and social insurance registration, THR holiday allowance, and minimum wage compliance (UMK) for the relevant Bali regency. Payroll is administered by us, and you receive a single transparent monthly fee.

**Q7: How much does it cost to hire staff through myCHEF.id?**
Staffing costs depend on the role, schedule, experience level, and the level of management required. We provide a fully transparent, itemized fee breakdown before you commit, with no hidden charges. Your monthly fee is all-inclusive: staff salary, BPJS, THR, ongoing management, and replacement coverage. Contact us for a confidential consultation and we will recommend the right setup for your villa.

**Q8: Can I interview candidates before making a decision?**
Absolutely. We coordinate interviews at your villa or via video call, and we provide interview guidance if you have never hired staff before. We strongly recommend a paid trial period, typically three to five days, where the candidate works under our supervision before you make a final commitment.

**Q9: Do you place temporary or event staff, or only permanent roles?**
We place both. Permanent placements such as villa managers, chefs, housekeepers, and drivers are our core service, but we also provide temporary event staff for weddings, private parties, corporate retreats, and peak-season coverage. All temporary staff go through the same vetting protocol as permanent placements.

**Q10: What areas of Bali do you cover?**
We place staff across all major villa regions: Seminyak, Canggu, Uluwatu, Jimbaran, Sanur, Nusa Dua, Ubud, and surrounding areas. For more remote locations such as Sidemen, North Bali, or the islands, additional sourcing time may be needed, but we have successfully placed staff in these areas.

**Q11: I am managing my villa from abroad. How do you handle communication and reporting?**
We are designed for remote owners. Your dedicated operations manager is your single point of contact, available via WhatsApp for daily updates, questions, and urgent issues. We provide monthly performance reports, attendance summaries, and incident documentation so you know what is happening without being in Bali.

**Q12: What makes myCHEF.id different from posting on Jobstreet or hiring directly?**
Jobstreet and direct hiring require you to handle sourcing, screening, interviews, identity and reference verification, contracts, BPJS, attendance management, and replacements yourself. myCHEF.id handles all of this professionally.
