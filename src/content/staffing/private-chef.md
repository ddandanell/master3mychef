## How We Find, Vet & Place Your Private Chef

Premium buyers expect process transparency. Our chef placement follows six clear steps, each designed to reduce risk and confirm fit before any long-term commitment.

### 1. Culinary Needs Assessment
We document your cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions, kitchen equipment, service style, and schedule before we search. Whether you need a family cook, a guest-facing rental villa chef, or an executive chef for multi-course tasting menus, the brief drives every decision.

### 2. Active Sourcing from Our Network
We do not post public job advertisements and wait. We actively recruit from hospitality professionals across Bali — chefs currently working in restaurants, hotels, private villas, and our own event catering operations. Because myCHEF also runs Bali's leading private dining and event catering service, we often know which chefs perform under pressure before they consider a new role.

### 3. Documented Verification Protocol
Every candidate completes our protocol before entering your home:

- **Identity verification** — Government-issued ID (KTP) reviewed and confirmed.
- **Work history verification** — Previous employers contacted directly; roles, dates, and responsibilities confirmed.
- **Reference and experience checks** — Minimum two professional references from hospitality supervisors.
- **Local police record review (SKCK)** — Current certificate from the candidate's local police district.
- **Skills assessment** — Practical cooking demonstration evaluated against your cuisine requirements.
- **Food safety certification review** — HACCP or equivalent food hygiene credentials verified.

Every step is conducted with candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law.

### 4. Curated Shortlist
You meet two or three chefs who have passed our protocol and match your needs. Each shortlist includes cuisine specializations, experience level, languages spoken, and why we believe they fit your villa.

### 5. Trial & Placement
We recommend a structured trial period — typically 3–5 days of live service — so you can assess culinary quality, personality fit, and guest interaction before committing. We maintain daily check-ins and collect your feedback in real time.

### 6. Ongoing Management & Support
After placement, your chef is employed by myCHEF.id. We manage payroll, BPJS, THR, proactive attendance monitoring, quarterly skills refreshers, and menu development support. If your chef is unavailable, we activate a trained backup roster briefed on your preferences.

## Private Chef Bali Placement Options for Every Villa & Lifestyle

Villa kitchens differ as much as the villas themselves. We place culinary professionals across a range of schedules, specializations, and service levels.

**Full-Time Live-In Chef**
For villas with frequent guests or large families, a live-in chef provides consistent coverage, manages kitchen inventory, plans weekly menus, and adapts to spontaneous requests. We handle accommodation, schedules, and professional boundaries.

**Full-Time Live-Out Chef**
For owners who prefer staff off-site, we place daily chefs who arrive each morning, prepare meals, and leave the kitchen spotless. This suits smaller villas and families who value privacy.

**Personal / Daily Family Chef**
Focused on household nutrition, these chefs specialize in healthy daily meal preparation, family-friendly menus, children's dietary needs, and efficient kitchen management. Popular with expat families in Canggu, Sanur, and Ubud.

**Executive Villa Chef**
For ultra-luxury estates, we place chefs with fine-dining, hotel, or international restaurant experience. They handle multi-course tasting menus, wine pairing, allergen management, and front-of-house guest interaction. Many are multilingual.

**Specialty Cuisine Chefs**
We place vegan and plant-based chefs, halal-certified chefs with full halal kitchen protocol knowledge, BBQ and grill specialists, Indonesian regional cuisine experts, and Japanese, Italian, Mediterranean, and Middle Eastern cuisine specialists.

**Temporary & Seasonal Coverage**
For peak season, holiday rentals, or coverage during your permanent chef's leave, we provide temporary chefs who complete the same verification protocol as permanent placements.

Many owners pair a chef with a [villa manager](/staffing/villa-manager) to handle scheduling, vendor coordination, and guest relations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How long does it take to place a private chef in my Bali villa?**

If you need urgent coverage, we can often provide a temporary chef within 48–72 hours while we conduct the full search for your permanent placement.

**Q2: How much does a private chef cost in Bali per month?**
Monthly chef compensation typically falls between IDR 6,000,000 for an experienced villa cook and IDR 15,000,000+ for an executive chef with international restaurant or hotel experience. Our management fee is a separate, transparent monthly amount that covers sourcing, vetting, legal employment, ongoing management, and replacement coverage. We provide a full written breakdown before any commitment.

**Q3: What is the difference between a private chef and a villa cook?**
A villa cook typically handles daily Indonesian and basic Western meals for a family or small group. A private chef brings broader culinary training, menu-planning capability, guest-facing professionalism, and experience with dietary restrictions, multi-course service, and fine-dining presentation. At myCHEF, we place both — the right title depends on your villa's guest profile and service expectations.

**Q4: Do you place chefs who can handle vegan, halal, or allergen-free menus?**
Yes. We actively source chefs with specific dietary expertise and verify their training before placement. For halal requirements, we place chefs with halal kitchen protocol knowledge. For severe allergies, we require documented allergen-management training and cross-contamination prevention protocols. Dietary accommodation is part of our initial needs assessment, not an afterthought.

**Q5: What food safety certifications do your chefs hold?**
We verify that every culinary placement holds current food safety and hygiene knowledge, including HACCP principles or equivalent certification. We also conduct refresher training on tropical-climate food handling — temperature control, seafood storage, cross-contamination prevention, and safe leftover management — before any chef enters your kitchen.

**Q6: What happens if my chef suddenly leaves or stops showing up?**
If a chef departs unexpectedly, we manage the entire transition: we notify you immediately, provide a trained replacement chef briefed on your preferences, and begin a permanent replacement search if needed. Because we employ your chef directly, you are not left dealing with termination, legal obligations, or rehiring alone. Our 30-day replacement guarantee also applies if a replacement is needed within the first month.

**Q7: Can I try a chef before committing to a long-term placement?**
Yes. We strongly recommend a structured trial period — typically 3–5 days of live service in your villa — before finalizing long-term placement. During the trial, we maintain daily check-ins with you and the chef, collect your feedback, and make adjustments. The trial is designed to confirm culinary quality, personality fit, and service chemistry before any long-term commitment.

**Q8: Do I employ the chef directly, or does myCHEF handle that?**
myCHEF.id is the legal employer of record for all placed chefs. We handle compliant contracts, BPJS health and social security registration, THR holiday allowance, minimum wage compliance, and payroll. You receive a single monthly invoice from myCHEF. This structure protects you from direct employer liability and ensures full legal compliance with Indonesian labor law.

**Q9: What areas of Bali do you cover for chef placement?**
We place chefs across all major Bali villa regions: Seminyak, Canggu, Ubud, Uluwatu, Sanur, Nusa Dua, Jimbaran, and surrounding areas. Our network includes chefs who live in or near each region, reducing commute time and improving reliability. For remote estates, we can arrange live-in placements to ensure full coverage.

**Q10: Can you place a chef for a temporary or seasonal need?**
Yes. We provide temporary chef coverage for peak rental seasons, holiday periods, special events, or coverage during your permanent chef's leave. All temporary chefs complete the same verification protocol as permanent placements and are briefed on your villa's preferences before arrival. Contact us with your dates and requirements.

**Q11: How do you find chefs that aren't just applying to job ads?**
We actively recruit from our network of hospitality professionals, including chefs currently working in Bali's restaurants, hotels, and private villas. We also identify talent through our event catering operations — when a chef performs exceptionally at a myCHEF-catered event, they enter our placement pipeline. This passive-talent approach means we often know about exceptional chefs before they are actively seeking new roles.

**Q12: What if I'm not satisfied with my chef's performance after placement?**
We conduct quarterly performance reviews and maintain open feedback channels with every client. If performance concerns arise, we provide coaching and additional training to the chef. If the fit remains unsuitable, our 30-day replacement guarantee applies, and we manage the transition to a better-matched candidate. Your satisfaction is our responsibility, not yours to solve alone.
