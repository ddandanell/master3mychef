## Section 2: Staff Recruitment Bali — Our 7-Step Protocol

Premium villa owners do not want vague promises. They want a named process with specific checks and clear timeframes. Our 7-step protocol is designed to remove guesswork at every stage — from the first conversation to the quarterly performance review six months later.

**Step 1 — Needs Assessment & Role Definition**
We do not search until we understand your villa. A four-bedroom family retreat in Sanur requires a different housekeeper than a ten-bedroom event estate in Uluwatu. A vegan-forward wellness villa in Ubud needs a different chef than a steak-and-seafood property in Seminyak. We define the role, schedule, language needs, guest profile, and cultural fit before we look at a single candidate.

**Step 2 — Talent Mapping & Sourcing**
We draw from our proprietary network of 500+ hospitality professionals across Bali — built through years of villa placements, private dining events, and chef referrals. We do not post a job ad and wait. We actively identify candidates who match your brief, including passive talent who are currently employed but open to the right opportunity.

**Step 3 — Initial Screening & Interview**
Every candidate is interviewed by a myCHEF staffing consultant with hospitality experience. We assess technical ability, communication style, attitude, stability, and cultural fit. We look for warning signs: unexplained gaps, job-hopping, vague references, or mismatched expectations about villa work.

**Step 4 — Verification Protocol**
This is where most agencies cut corners. We do not. Every candidate completes identity verification via government-issued KTP, employment history confirmation with previous employers, a minimum of two professional reference checks from hospitality supervisors, a current Police Clearance Certificate (SKCK) from their local district, a role-specific skills assessment, and certification review where applicable. Every step is conducted with candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law.

**Step 5 — Curated Shortlist**
We do not hand you a stack of resumes. We present two or three carefully matched candidates with a clear summary of why each fits your brief, their verified experience, and our consultant's recommendation.

**Step 6 — Trial Placement & Onboarding**
The selected candidate begins a 30-day trial placement with enhanced oversight. We provide structured onboarding, clear service standards, and daily check-ins during the first two weeks.

**Step 7 — Ongoing Management & Performance Review**
After the trial, we transition to ongoing management: quarterly performance reviews, attendance monitoring, skills refreshers, and proactive replacement when needed. We do not place and disappear.

## Section 3: What "Vetted" Actually Means at myCHEF

Handing over keys to your villa, your valuables, and your family's safety is not something you do lightly. Neither do we. The word "vetted" is used casually across the industry, but we believe you deserve to know exactly what we check.

**Identity Verification:** We review and verify the candidate's government-issued ID (KTP) to confirm that their identity matches their application and employment history claims.

**Employment History Confirmation:** We contact previous employers directly — not only the references the candidate provides. We verify dates, roles, responsibilities, and reasons for departure, looking for patterns of reliability and growth.

**Professional Reference Checks:** We require a minimum of two references from hospitality supervisors who managed the candidate directly. We ask specific questions about punctuality, work quality, communication, guest interaction, and trustworthiness.

**Local Police Record Review (SKCK):** We require a current Surat Keterangan Catatan Kepolisian from the candidate's local police district. This confirms the candidate is not currently involved in criminal proceedings in that district. We are transparent about its limits: SKCK is district-specific, valid for six months, and must be obtained by the candidate themselves. There is no centralized national criminal database in Indonesia, so we never claim otherwise.

**Skills Assessment:** Every candidate demonstrates their abilities in context. Chefs prepare a trial menu. Housekeepers complete a quality audit. Villa managers run a scenario-based assessment. Bartenders mix a service sequence.

**Certification Review:** For culinary roles, we verify current HACCP or food safety knowledge. For technical and supervisory roles, we verify relevant credentials are current and issued by recognized institutions.

This documented verification protocol is applied to every candidate who enters your villa — not most, not almost. Every single one.

Hiring staff in Indonesia involves obligations that many foreign villa owners do not discover until there is a problem. BPJS health and employment insurance. THR religious holiday bonuses. Minimum wage compliance (UMK) that changes by regency. Contracts that must be in Bahasa Indonesia. These are not optional extras; they are legal requirements.

myCHEF.id employs your staff directly. We are the legal employer of record. We handle contracts, payroll, benefits, and compliance. You are the client, not the employer.

- **Compliant Contracts:** PKWTT for permanent staff, PKWT for fixed-term, drafted in Bahasa Indonesia.
- **BPJS Registration & Payment:** Health and employment insurance registered and paid on time.
- **THR:** Calculated and paid according to Indonesian law.
- **Minimum Wage Compliance:** Staff paid at or above UMK for their regency, with automatic adjustments when rates change.
- **Payroll Administration:** Monthly salary, overtime, and leave tracking — you receive one invoice.
- **Dispute Resolution:** Labor issues are handled by us, not by you.

Cash-in-hand and informal arrangements may feel simpler, but they expose owners to fines, disputes, and reputational damage. With myCHEF, your staffing is fully compliant, fully documented, and fully protected.

## Section 9: Frequently Asked Questions About Staff Recruitment in Bali

**Q1: How long does the recruitment process take?**
Most placements are completed within 2–4 weeks. Simple roles such as housekeepers or gardeners can be placed faster; specialized roles such as executive chefs or villa managers may take 3–6 weeks. We do not rush a placement to meet a deadline — the right fit matters more than speed.

**Q2: What checks do you actually perform on candidates?**
Every candidate completes our documented verification protocol: identity verification via KTP, employment history confirmation with previous employers, a minimum of two professional reference checks, current local police record review (SKCK), role-specific skills assessment, and certification review where applicable. All checks are conducted with candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesian data protection law.

**Q3: What happens if the placed staff member doesn't work out?**
Every placement includes a 30-day trial period with enhanced oversight. If the fit is not right within the first 90 days, we provide a replacement at no additional placement fee. We manage the transition and onboarding of the new candidate.

**Q4: Can I interview candidates before making a decision?**
Yes. We present a curated shortlist of 2–3 matched candidates. You may interview them in person or by video call, and you can request a trial cooking session for chefs or a trial shift for other roles.

**Q5: Are you the legal employer, or am I?**
myCHEF.id is the legal employer of record. We employ your staff directly, handle contracts, payroll, BPJS, THR, and compliance. You are the client, not the employer.

**Q6: What roles can you recruit for?**
We specialize in villa hospitality staffing: private chefs, villa managers, housekeepers, butlers, bartenders, waiters, gardeners, pool technicians, maintenance staff, security, and drivers. We also provide temporary event staff and cover staff. For details, see our [villa staff page](/staffing/villa-staff).

**Q7: How much does it cost to recruit staff through myCHEF?**
Staffing costs depend on the role, schedule, experience level, and level of management required. Our fee is all-inclusive: staff salary, BPJS health and social insurance, THR holiday bonus, ongoing management, and replacement coverage. There are no hidden charges. Contact us for a confidential consultation and transparent fee structure.

**Q8: What if my staff member is sick or needs time off?**
We maintain a trained standby pool for every role we place. If your regular team member is unavailable, we provide a pre-briefed replacement with same-role experience, so your service continues without interruption.

**Q9: Do you offer trial periods?**
Yes. Every placement includes a 30-day trial with daily check-ins in week one, weekly check-ins through day 30, and a formal review at the end. This gives you real-world experience with the candidate before committing long-term.

**Q10: How do you handle cultural differences between Western owners and Balinese staff?**
Our Bali-based management team serves as the cultural bridge between you and your staff. We translate your priorities into locally appropriate guidance, accommodate scheduling around ceremonial obligations such as Galungan, and manage communication styles with respect for both cultures.

**Q11: What is an SKCK, and is it enough to ensure safety?**
SKCK (Surat Keterangan Catatan Kepolisian) is a Police Clearance Certificate issued by the candidate's local district. It confirms the candidate is not currently involved in criminal proceedings in that district. It is district-specific, valid for six months, and is one layer of our verification protocol — not the whole story. We supplement it with identity verification, employment history confirmation, reference checks, and skills assessment.

**Q12: Can you replace staff I already have, or only place new staff?**
We can do both. If you have existing staff who are underperforming or have left, we can recruit replacements. If you are opening a new villa or expanding your team, we can build your full roster from scratch. We also offer staff evaluations to identify gaps or training needs in your current team.
