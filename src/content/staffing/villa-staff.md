### Section 4: Our Vetting & Placement Protocol

## How We Verify Every Villa Staff Member Before They Enter Your Home

Letting someone into your private villa — around your family, your guests, and your valuables — requires a process, not a promise. Every candidate we place completes the same documented verification protocol, conducted with candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022).

1. **Identity verification** — Government-issued ID (KTP) is reviewed and matched against the candidate's application and work history.
2. **Work-history verification** — Previous employers are contacted directly to confirm dates, roles, responsibilities, and reason for departure.
3. **Professional reference checks** — We speak with at least two hospitality supervisors or former employers, asking specifically about reliability, quality, communication, and conduct.
4. **Police clearance (SKCK)** — We require a current Surat Keterangan Catatan Kepolisian from the candidate's local police district, confirming no active criminal proceedings. SKCK is district-specific and valid for six months; it does not claim a lifetime record.
5. **Skills assessment** — Candidates demonstrate role-specific competency: a housekeeper completes a quality audit, a chef prepares a trial menu, a villa manager walks through a guest-arrival scenario.
6. **Certification review** — Culinary candidates must show current food-safety knowledge; other roles are checked for relevant training credentials.
7. **Supervised trial period** — The first weeks include enhanced oversight with structured feedback at 7, 14, and 30 days, so fit is tested in real villa conditions.

We do not claim "full national background checks" — Indonesia does not have a centralised national criminal database. We do not claim "100% verified." We claim that every candidate completes our documented verification protocol before entering your home.
