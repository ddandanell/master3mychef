**Reliability and attendance gaps.** A housekeeper misses a morning before a guest arrival. A gardener disappears during rainy season. A chef calls in sick on the night of a villa dinner party. When you are managing from Sydney, London, or Singapore, these problems reach you at the worst possible hour — and you are the one left scrambling for cover.

**The trust question.** Handing keys and alarm codes to someone you barely know is deeply personal. BPJS contributions, THR holiday bonuses, minimum wage compliance (UMK), and proper contracts in Bahasa Indonesia are not optional details — they are legal obligations. Getting them wrong exposes owners to fines, disputes, and damaged relationships.

## How We Verify Every Villa Team Member — Before They Enter Your Home

Trust is not assumed. It is built through a structured, documented verification protocol that every candidate completes before their first day on your property. We do not use vague claims like "comprehensive checks." We use specific, legally accurate steps.

1. **Identity Verification.** Every candidate's government-issued ID (KTP) is reviewed and confirmed against their application and stated work history.

2. **Work History Verification.** We contact previous employers directly to confirm dates, roles, responsibilities, and reasons for departure. Stated hospitality experience is validated through direct outreach.

3. **Reference and Experience Checks.** We obtain a minimum of two professional references from hospitality supervisors and ask specific questions about reliability, quality, guest interaction, and reason for leaving.

4. **Local Police Record Review (SKCK).** We require a current Surat Keterangan Catatan Kepolisian from the candidate's local police district, confirming no active criminal proceedings at the time of placement. SKCK is district-specific and valid for six months — we ensure it is current.

5. **Skills Assessment.** Every candidate completes a practical, role-specific evaluation. Chefs prepare a tasting menu. Housekeepers complete a quality audit. Villa managers work through guest-scenario problem-solving. Maintenance staff demonstrate tool competence and tropical-climate knowledge.

6. **Certification Review.** Food safety, hygiene, and role-specific credentials are verified. For culinary roles, we confirm current food safety understanding. For technical roles, we verify training certificates.

7. **Supervised Trial Period.** Every placement begins with a structured trial period under enhanced supervision. We gather feedback from you, your guests, and our operations manager before confirming long-term placement.

All verification is conducted with candidate consent and in compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). We do not promise risk-free staffing — we promise systematic risk reduction through a documented verification protocol.

For a full breakdown of every step, see [our recruitment and vetting process](/staffing/recruitment).
